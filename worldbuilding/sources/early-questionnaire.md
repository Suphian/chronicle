# **Backstory**

Hanno Averroes, a half-elf merchant from North Africa, built a reputation in the underground trade world. Born into a family of traders, Hanno's father was a small-time trader of fruits and vegetables, operating a modest fruit cart. Unfortunately, due to overleveraging himself, he went bankrupt and was forced into indentured servitude. Hanno's world crumbled as his father labored day and night on a farm for the wealthy Vassal Lord Namarius. The family was taxed so heavily that Hanno's mother, Amara, had to make pottery and sell it on weekends to make ends meet. She also managed to secure an apprenticeship for Hanno with an apothecary, where he discovered his gift for potion-making.

The apothecary's owner took advantage of Hanno and profited immensely from his innovative work, while Hanno saw very little in return. Hanno was sent to travel the world and bring rare ingredients back to his master, which is where he learned his diverse weapon skills and made valuable connections.  As Hanno grew older, he quickly realized that wealth and power could be attained through the right connections, leading him to cultivate a network of scouts, alchemists, and wizards.

As Hanno continued to expand his network, he found himself traveling the world to collect rare ingredients and establish valuable connections. He became a true cosmopolitan, absorbing diverse skills and techniques from the cultures he encountered.

In the vast Mongolian steppes, Hanno learned the art of horse archery, becoming a deadly marksman with a bow and arrow. During his time in Japan, he trained with skilled samurai, mastering the way of the sword. And in Macedonia, he honed his skills with the spear, adopting the tactics of the legendary phalanx.

Hanno's cunning mind allowed him to combine his combat prowess with his potion-making abilities. He coated his blades, arrows, and spear tips with potent concoctions that could incapacitate or disorient his opponents, giving him a significant advantage in combat. He also developed unique elixirs that temporarily granted him superhuman strength or heightened reflexes, allowing him to dodge attacks and move with incredible speed.

Tragically, Hanno's father eventually passed away in the fields and was heartlessly buried there, his body used as fertilizer by the evil vassal. Hanno only learned about this after returning from his travels. He found his mother homeless on the streets, unrecognizable and addicted to a dangerous street drug called "Ravash." The drug was so addictive that just one puff could lead to a lifetime of dependence, and Hanno's master had given it to his mother to ensure Hanno's perpetual servitude.

Enraged, Hanno killed the apothecary master and spent months trying to cure his mother. He eventually found the cure and began selling it, starting his business empire and earning respect as the savior of Carthara. Hanno managed to end the Ravash epidemic that had plagued Carthara, winning him the favor of Lord Numarius and the gratitude of the townspeople.

Over the years, Hanno's business flourished, and he amassed wealth beyond his wildest dreams. His mother was cured of her Ravash addiction, but she was never the same. The long-term effects of the drug left her with trembling hands, bouts of memory loss, and periods of severe anxiety. Although the vassal had no idea who Hanno was, Hanno had not forgotten the cruel treatment his family had endured at the hands of the vassal. He bided his time, patiently waiting for the perfect opportunity to exact his revenge on the man who had callously left his father to rot in the fields.

Determined to undermine Lord Numarius's success, Hanno sought to create an even more addictive substance that would devastate the Kingdom of Carthara and allow him to profit from drug sales. He traveled the world, searching far and wide for the perfect ingredients to concoct this destructive substance.

During his travels, Hanno found himself in the region of Lysandria, known for its skilled spear fighters. There, he spent five years training with the Lysandrian Phalanx, a warrior society similar to the Spartans. Under their tutelage, Hanno honed his skills. His weapon of choice was a double-sided spear called "Zaharaz."  "Zaharaz" is a word derived from an ancient language spoken by the Lysandrian Phalanx. It symbolizes the concept of balance and harmony, reflecting the double-sided nature of the spear. The palindrome name emphasizes the importance of equilibrium in both attack and defense, a principle that the Lysandrian Phalanx holds in high regard.

The name also has a deeper meaning related to the duality of life and death. As a weapon, the Zaharaz spear can bring both protection and destruction, depending on the intentions of its wielder. This duality resonates with Hanno's own life, as he uses his skills and knowledge to create both healing potions and destructive substances. The Zaharaz spear serves as a reminder of the choices he makes and the consequences that come with them.

In Lysandria, Hanno met his wife, Alethea, a skilled healer and herbalist with a strong Spartan heritage. On their wedding night, he placed a flower in her hair, a yellow and red plant that was abundant in the region. This flower contained the compound he needed to create the highly addictive substance he had been seeking. After years of research and experimentation, Hanno finally succeeded in formulating the potent drug \-- “Elysian Dust.”

Upon returning to Carthara, Hanno devised an ingenious way to distribute Elysian Dust without anyone knowing it was connected to him. He created a network of seemingly unrelated merchants, each selling a variety of legitimate goods, but with the addictive substance secretly hidden within. The merchants themselves were unaware of the true nature of their cargo, believing they were simply transporting harmless products. This intricate system allowed Hanno to supply the Elysian Dust discreetly, maintaining his reputation as a respected merchant while undermining Lord Numarius's power.

Elysian Dust spread throughout Carthara and beyond, and its devastating effects became increasingly apparent. The once-prosperous kingdom began to crumble under the weight of addiction and despair. Lord Numarius’s standing in the king’s court falling. Families were torn apart, and once-thriving communities fell into disarray. 

In the midst of this chaos, Hanno remained a respected figure, with no one suspecting his involvement in the crisis. He kept his activities hidden even from his wife, Alethea, who continued to love and support him, completely unaware of the darkness that consumed her husband's heart.

For Hanno, witnessing the world in turmoil brought him a twisted sense of satisfaction. The pain and suffering he had experienced in his life were now being felt by others, and in his mind, it was a fitting revenge against the injustice he had faced. But deep down, a lingering doubt began to gnaw at his conscience, forcing him to question the path he had chosen and the legacy he would leave behind.
