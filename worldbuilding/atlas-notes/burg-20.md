# Chalcis

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The ringing lane:** Metalworkers test bells in a courtyard where customers learn to recognize repairs by ear.
- **A common measure:** The same shops make household balances and ceremonial fittings, giving practical skill an audience beyond wealthy patrons.
- **The alloy dispute:** A supplier delivers inconsistent stock; proving the fault means risking the next shipment.
