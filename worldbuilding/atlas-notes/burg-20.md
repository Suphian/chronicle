# Chalcis

- **Area (proposal):** A mountain town among conifer woods in northwestern Zorig, with stone terraces, timber halls, and sheltered foundries.
- **Control (proposal):** Erdenechimeg Mergen the Fifth governs the district under the Zorig crown. Town households retain their property and local assemblies.
- **Importance (proposal):** A center for metalworking and mountain provisioning; its foundries depend on managed woodland and imported supplies.
