# Tserendorj Mergen the Fifth

- **Role (source lore):** A named later Mergen figure associated with the family's settlement in Zorig.
- **Authority (proposal):** Lord of the Melameaion upland district, owing service to King Jirgal the Fifth while retaining local civil jurisdiction.
- **Seat (proposal):** Melameaion's citadel. The adjacent settlement and person markers support considering this connection, but do not establish the fief as existing canon.
