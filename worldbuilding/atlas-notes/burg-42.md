# Oncheracra

- **Area (proposal):** A forested highland city in southern Ruhania, with steep gardens, sheltered courts, and warehouses built into terraces.
- **Control (proposal):** Highland Steward Tarek Solan governs for Queen Samira Daran; local terrace councils manage water channels and retaining works.
- **Importance (proposal):** A collection center for upland produce, medicinal plants, and timber, connecting the highlands to Ruhania's larger markets.
