# Hikari no Kuni

- **Area (source lore):** A northeastern kingdom of the Hikarujin, with restrained architecture, secluded monasteries, and pilgrimage centered on Kōmyōsan, the Mountain of Radiance.
- **Control (proposal):** Sovereign Ayame Seiran governs with a council of provincial stewards. The Hikari Kenshi defend the realm; monastic authorities maintain the principal pilgrimage sites.
- **Notable places (map and source lore):** Redclesbury, Castford, and Byreum are mapped settlements. Kōmyōsan is the central sacred mountain; the source's claims about divine light remain religious beliefs and magical proposals.
