# Brother Oyuunchimeg

- **Role (source lore):** A named member of the older Mergen resistance leadership.
- **Authority (proposal):** Coordinator of eastern refuges and household assistance, working through local representatives rather than appointing civil governors.
- **Base (proposal):** The wooded heights east of Zorig city. No territorial grant from Jirgal is yet adopted for this individual.
