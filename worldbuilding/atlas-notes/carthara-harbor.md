# Corvo's Harbor

- **Area (current draft):** Carthara's docks and customs district, with working berths, quays, and bonded storage handling the city's sea trade.
- **Control (current draft):** Magistra Ilvane Corvo, the Second Seat, holds harbor and customs authority. Her office controls duties and access to berths; it does not imply ownership of every ship or cargo.
- **Importance (current draft):** The city's maritime entrance and a major source of customs revenue. Its maintenance depends on levies negotiated with the other Seats.
