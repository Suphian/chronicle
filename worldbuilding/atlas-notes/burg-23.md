# Paphidnapon

- **Area (proposal):** A high northern town in Zorig's tundra country, with compact stone buildings, steep approaches, and large winter stores.
- **Control (proposal):** Bilegt Mergen governs a vassal district here. The source disagrees over whether Bilegt is the Third or Fifth, so the ordinal remains unsettled.
- **Importance (proposal):** A defensive and provisioning center for northern upland routes, dependent on food brought from lower settlements.
