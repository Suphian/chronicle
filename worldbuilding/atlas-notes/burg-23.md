Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The lamp court:** Families bring broken lamps to an evening repair market, trading gossip while they wait for the light.
- **The ornament shop:** Pierced brass shades scatter elaborate patterns over modest rooms.
- **The price of a standard:** A merchant wants identical fittings across all lamps; makers welcome easier repairs but dispute ownership of the design.
