# Qa'hir Sands

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The departure cloth:** Hosts spread a patterned cloth to sort water vessels, food, and personal luggage before travelers leave.
- **The shade lenders:** Courtyard households rent resting places while keeping a few spaces available for arrivals without sponsors.
- **A misplaced name:** The map marks a settlement where the geography notes name a desert region; its connection to Qalb Al-Sahra remains open.
