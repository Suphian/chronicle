# Qa'hir Sands

- **Area (source lore):** The Qa'hir is Ruhania's eastern desert, with dunes, rocky outcrops, and oases supporting palms, olives, and settled trade.
- **Control (proposal):** Desert Governor Idrane Qasem administers the province for Queen Samira Daran from Qalb Al-Sahra; oasis councils regulate local water access.
- **Map identity (open question):** The source map marks “Qa'hir Sands” as a settlement. Whether that point is Qalb Al-Sahra or a separate desert town remains undecided.
