# The Moonlit Isle

- **Area (current draft):** A secluded island off Lysandria, where the red-and-yellow Elysian flower grows and Hanno and Alethea marry.
- **Control (open question):** No owner, custodian, or governing authority is established. Its proximity to Lysandria does not by itself prove that it is Phylios's private property.
- **Importance (current draft):** The flower links the island to healing, the couple's marriage, and the later Dust trade. Harvest rights, permanent inhabitants, and the exact map position remain to be developed.
