# Adiyabold Mergen the Fifth

- **Role (source lore):** A named later Mergen figure whose marker lies beside Zakra.
- **Authority (proposal):** Lord of Zakra and its surrounding mountain district, owing service to the Zorig crown while maintaining a local garrison.
- **Seat (proposal):** Zakra's fortified upper town. This proposed fief does not resolve the earlier Mergen exile chronology.
