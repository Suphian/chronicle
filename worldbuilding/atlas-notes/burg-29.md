# Domis

- **Area (proposal):** A rain-soaked woodland town of deep-roofed houses, covered lanes, and timber yards.
- **Control (proposal):** A council of resident households elects a town provost; the source's unnamed region leaves any greater sovereign unresolved.
- **Importance (proposal):** Timber processing, furniture making, and repair workshops serving scattered settlements in the surrounding forest.
