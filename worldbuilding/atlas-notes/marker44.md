# Arban Naran

- **Role (source lore):** Leader of the Uulyn-Khökh Tribe, associated with a mountain settlement and tunnel trade near Majlis Gate.
- **Authority (source lore):** Local tribal leadership and influence over the community's trading connections; the source does not make him owner of Majlis Gate.
- **Territory (open question):** The mountain settlement, its routes, and its current political status need reconciliation with Leonin displacement and Catharan jurisdiction.
