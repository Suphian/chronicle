# Bingraleton

- **Area (proposal):** A compact town at the wooded edge of V W's colder country, built around communal stores and sheltered winter courts.
- **Control (proposal):** Warden Veyra Sen presides over the territory's household council here. The town maintains its own civil officers.
- **Importance (proposal):** The territory's main winter provisioning and trade center, linking woodland settlements with tundra households.
