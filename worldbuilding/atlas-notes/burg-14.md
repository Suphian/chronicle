# Titanus

- **Area (source lore):** The capital of Tharsis, the Isle of Strength: a rugged landscape of cliffs and rocky ground associated with warriors, hunters, and athletic competition.
- **Control (proposal):** Marshal Damaris Theron governs Titanus through an assembly of household and military representatives. Any allegiance to Phylios remains undecided.
- **Landmark (source lore):** The Coliseum of Titans hosts the annual Tournament of Titans. The map groups Titanus with Lysandria, while the prose places it on Tharsis; the island relationship remains open.
