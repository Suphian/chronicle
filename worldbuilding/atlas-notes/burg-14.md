# Titanus

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **Beyond the arena:** Laundries, kitchens, and equipment workshops make a tournament possible; their workers expect payment before the spectacle begins.
- **An injured contender:** A celebrated athlete wants to compete, but the healer who must certify fitness refuses the patron's pressure.
- **The stone yard:** Retired competitors could teach lifting and balance to builders, turning a public reputation into an ordinary second livelihood.

**Open continuity:** The source note describes Tharsis as an island; the atlas label is Titanus. Their relationship remains open.
