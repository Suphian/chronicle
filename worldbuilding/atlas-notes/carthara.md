# Carthara

- **Area (current draft):** A southern coastal trade city of crowded markets, workshops, wealthy houses, and a busy harbor. Numarius's plantations lie east of its walls.
- **Control (current draft):** The Three Seats: Lord Numarius holds the fields, Magistra Ilvane Corvo the harbor, and General Tarquin Vael the garrison.
- **Landmarks (current draft):** Corvo's customs house and docks, Vael's guard barracks, the markets, and Alethea's clinic. This coastal city is distinct from the source map's inland capital Tijara.
