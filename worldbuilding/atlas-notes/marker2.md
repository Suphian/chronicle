# Bahar Oasis

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The water steps:** Households gather with differently shaped jars, recognizing familiar neighbors before they reach the front of the queue.
- **The keeper's chalk:** A proposed daily tally makes withdrawals visible to bathhouses, gardeners, and residents alike.
- **The queen's gift:** Storytellers disagree over what Queen Al Zahra's legendary gift requires of those who charge others for water; the legend does not establish a magical supply.
