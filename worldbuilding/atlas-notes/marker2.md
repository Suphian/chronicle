# Bahar Oasis

- **Area (source lore):** The oasis around which Tijara grew, providing the water and cultivated ground that sustain the capital amid the desert.
- **Control (proposal):** A water conservancy holds the springs in public trust under Tijara's governor. Household and agricultural allotments are administered separately from the city's commercial tolls.
- **Landmarks (source lore and proposal):** The source associates its origin with Queen Al Zahra and desert spirits. Palm gardens, public draw-wells, and a protected spring court are proposed physical features.
