# Lysandria

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The evening terraces:** Potters stack drying bowls beneath pierced screens while apprentices carry supper between workshops; a kiln firing is a neighborhood appointment.
- **The price of metal:** Smiths want reliable ore deliveries, while carriers want the mountain road repaired before another loaded cart leaves.
- **Two mountains, many loyalties:** Harbor households, inland growers, and soldiers argue over island needs without sharing a single view of Phylios.
