# Lysandria

- **Area (current draft):** An island east of the mainland, dominated by two enormous mountains. Its inhabited slopes connect harbors, pottery workshops, healing rooms, and metalworking settlements.
- **Control (current draft):** General Phylios rules Lysandria. His authority is political and military; it does not mean he personally owns every town or workshop.
- **Notable places (source lore):** Aegis stands beside Harmony Lagoon. The Spear of Poseidon Peak and Elysium Fields are named landmarks; Titanus, Pallas, and Lustria also appear in the island's map region, with their older island identities unresolved.
