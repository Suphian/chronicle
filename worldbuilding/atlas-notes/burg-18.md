# Zorig

- **Area (proposal):** The crown city of Zorig, built on high forested ground around a many-courted palace and terraced residential districts.
- **Control (proposal):** King Jirgal the Fifth holds court here. Crown officers administer the city and surrounding royal lands, while provincial lords govern outlying districts.
- **Landmarks (proposal):** The royal palace, an audience court for vassal households, and a fortified storehouse complex serving the capital.
