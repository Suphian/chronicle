# Ruhania

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The two-market parcel:** Traders pair woven wetland baskets with jars of preserved desert fruit, making an ordinary gift out of the region's different livelihoods.
- **Water remembered:** Jahnavi boat crews keep flood recollections beside their cargo accounts; growers dispute whose memory should guide the next season's works.
- **Many tongues at supper:** A lodging courtyard hosts translators, fish sellers, and visiting students. Its Black proprietor knows which interpreters charge fairly and which promise fluency they lack.
