# Ruhania

- **Area (source lore):** A kingdom divided by the Jahnavi River: the Qa'hir desert and oasis settlements to the east, the Zulimar wetlands to the west. The Safar Peaks rise to the north; the Azure Sea lies westward in the geography notes.
- **Control (proposal):** Queen Samira Daran rules through two provincial administrations: Qalb Al-Sahra governs the desert districts, and Al-Rutbah governs the wetlands. Local water councils oversee irrigation and waterways.
- **Notable places (source lore):** Al-Rutbah is a city of stilts and canals containing the Grand Codexium. Other landmarks include Zephyr Pass, the Tower of Zulfaar, the buried ruins of Al-Qamar Al-Khafiq, and the sunken gardens of Jannat Al-Rutbah.
