# Pallas

- **Area (source lore):** The capital of Sophia, the Isle of Wisdom, surrounded by olive groves and clear brooks and centered on scholarly institutions.
- **Control (proposal):** Chancellor Ione Sere chairs a civic council of residents and learned institutions. The library has its own custodians and is not the chancellor's private collection.
- **Landmark (source lore):** The Grand Library. Its Sophia island setting and the map's Lysandrian grouping remain unreconciled.
