# Sidrat Al Muntaha

- **Area (current draft):** A luminous land of sacred lakes, moss-covered bridges, and roads edged with bioluminescent plants. Lake Siraj is Hanno's principal narrated stop.
- **Control (proposal):** Queen Safiya Nural governs with district Keepers; Jibreel, proposed Warden of the Sidrat Web, oversees roads and bridges. These offices are new outline proposals.
- **Landmarks (source lore):** The seven lakes, the Veins of Sidrat, the summit temple of Jabal Al-Nur, and the frontier fortress of Jabal Al-Hifaz.
