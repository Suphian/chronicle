# Erdenechimeg Mergen the Fifth

- **Role (source lore):** A later Mergen figure whose marker lies beside Chalcis in Zorig.
- **Authority (proposal):** Lord of the Chalcis district under Jirgal, overseeing local defense, foundry duties, and obligations owed by the district.
- **Seat (proposal):** A fortified hall above Chalcis's workshops. The district assignment is new; the source supplies the person and location association.
