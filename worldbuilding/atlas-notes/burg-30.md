# Menos

- **Area (proposal):** The chief town of the region labeled Fairy, built across wetland clearings with raised homes, garden platforms, and narrow footbridges.
- **Control (proposal):** A local grove council administers Menos within Lady Neris Thale's proposed woodland compact.
- **Importance (proposal):** The region's market and nursery center, exchanging cultivated plants and forest products with outsiders.
