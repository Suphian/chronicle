Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The small gardens:** Balconies overflow with carefully trained plants, exchanged through cuttings at a weekly courtyard market.
- **The patient craft:** Growers teach buyers how to keep their purchases alive, charging for return visits rather than impossible promises.
- **The collector's demand:** A foreign buyer wants every specimen of a rare local variety; growers debate what should remain available to neighbors.
