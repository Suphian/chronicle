# Thege

- **Area (proposal):** A wooded highland city in northeastern Yáolán, with rain-sheltered lanes and dense craft quarters.
- **Control (proposal):** An imperial governor holds the district; forest communities administer their own common woodland within the proposed provincial charter.
- **Importance (proposal):** Timber, woven goods, and forest products, with warehouses serving the eastern provinces.
