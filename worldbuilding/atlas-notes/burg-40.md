# Thege

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The reed workshop:** Weavers turn purchased reeds into shutters, baskets, and screens with patterns customers recognize across the market.
- **A courtyard concert:** Instrument players test new reeds while craftspeople work, making an ordinary afternoon briefly festive.
- **The bulk buyer:** A large order offers steady work but demands one standard pattern and a price small makers cannot sustain.
