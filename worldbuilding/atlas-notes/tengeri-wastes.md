# The Tengeri Wastes

- **Area (current draft):** Wind-carved canyons and refugee camps southwest of Carthara, sheltering displaced Leonin under repeated bombardment and forced labor.
- **Control (current draft):** The power associated with Numarius restricts movement and labor. Camp institutions administer civilian needs within those constraints; their independence is severely limited.
- **Local leadership (current draft):** Chuluun teaches and leads part of the resistance while remaining subject to a labor account. The Return Councils and other Leonin networks represent different interests and do not share one ruler.
