Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The lamp round:** Market tenants share the cost of keeping common passages lit, arguing over which lanes receive attention first.
- **The blocked passage:** A large trader stores goods beyond his rented frontage, narrowing the path used by porters and neighbors.
