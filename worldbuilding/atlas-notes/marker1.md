# Tijara · the cavern markets

- **Area (source lore):** The underground commercial heart of Tijara, connected to the Souq Caverns beneath the desert realm.
- **Control (proposal):** The city's merchant magistrates license stalls and warehouses; Governor Farid Nassar appoints the market watch. This proposed office does not settle the coastal city's government.
- **Importance (source lore):** Sheltered trading passages allow commerce to continue through the hottest part of the day and connect the oasis capital with the wider cavern network.
