# V B

- **Area (proposal):** A rainy southern territory of wooded lowlands, sheltered estates, and dense settlements around Hemaris. Timber and enclosed cultivation support its towns.
- **Control (proposal):** Governor Sava Orun administers the territory from Hemaris, supported by landed households and town representatives. Its relationship to a greater sovereign remains open.
- **Name and landmarks (open question):** The source supplies the initials “V B,” not an expanded name. Hemaris is mapped; individual estates, ports, and borders of ownership still need placement.
