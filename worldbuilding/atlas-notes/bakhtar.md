# Bakhtar

- **Area (current draft):** Highlands north of Carthara, with cliff villages, enclosed valleys, caves, and difficult mountain approaches.
- **Control (proposal):** Independent valley councils and local strongholds, with no adopted ruler over all Bakhtar. Pass agreements connect communities without making them a single kingdom.
- **Identity (current draft):** The setting of the contested Iskandar and Rukhsana legend, known for cliff settlement and flute traditions. Amara's earlier ancestral connection remains unresolved; her home is Lysandria.
