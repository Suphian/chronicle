# Yáolán

- **Area (proposal):** A broad imperial realm of dry interior basins, grassland districts, and wooded eastern provinces. Its cities are linked by administrative roads, grain depots, and fortified tax stations.
- **Control (proposal):** Empress Wen Suyin rules through provincial governors and an imperial treasury. Her court claims tribute from subordinate rulers while leaving much local administration in their hands.
- **Reach (source lore):** The early Elarion and Numarius notes identify Yáolán as Cathara's imperial overlord. Whether that relationship survives in the novel's present remains unresolved.
