# Yáolán

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **Public grandeur:** Mosaic promenades display portraits of provincial benefactors, but cleaners know which donors have stopped paying the maintenance fund.
- **Traveling standards:** Inspectors bring official weights in velvet-lined cases. Local traders want the inspectors' own measures checked in public.
- **An empire on paper:** A proposed survey office records overlapping claims without proving that the court can enforce them; a beautiful map may promise more authority than its owner possesses.
