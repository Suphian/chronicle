# Brother Orgilmaa

- **Role (source lore):** One of the nine Mergen descendants associated with the northern resistance story.
- **Authority (proposal):** Representative in negotiations with Zorig's crown and provincial households, managing agreements for refuge and provisions.
- **Base (proposal):** The southern approaches to Zorig city, with access dependent on hosts rather than ownership of a court estate.
