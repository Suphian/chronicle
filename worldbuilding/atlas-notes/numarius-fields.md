# The Fields of Numarius

- **Area (current draft):** Extensive plantations east of Carthara, worked by people compelled through debt and held under coercive labor arrangements.
- **Control (current draft):** Lord Numarius owns the estates and exercises power through agents, managers, and labor accounts. Idris Averroes is taken here after losing his stall.
- **Production (open question):** The exact main crop remains undecided; a staple grain is under consideration. Irrigation, acreage, and individual estate boundaries have not been fixed.
