# Barsbold Khulan

- **Role (source lore):** Leader of the Tsagaan-Amgalan Tribe, described as resisting occupation in territory north of Erdene Zuu.
- **Authority (source lore):** Leadership of his own community and resistance forces, not sovereignty over the entire Tengeri region.
- **Territory (open question):** The source places his people in an encircled district. How that district relates to present camps, the lost homeland, and Numarius's power remains to be decided.
