# Braurias

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The disputing school:** Teachers hold public arguments in a courtyard where listeners can leave questions on clay tablets.
- **The book kitchen:** A printer and a cook share premises, feeding readers who wait for copied pages.
- **An unwanted patron:** A donor offers protection from hostile outsiders in exchange for influence over the lessons.
