# Braurias

- **Area (proposal):** A grassland city of fortified residential wards, public schools, and a central assembly court.
- **Control (proposal):** The League of Free Houses meets here under Speaker Maelin Rusk. The town's own council manages its civil affairs.
- **Importance (proposal):** Proposed political center of the region labeled Heretics, providing a meeting ground for otherwise autonomous towns.
