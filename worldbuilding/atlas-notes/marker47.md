# Ilm Ki Dunya · the Grand Codexium

- **Area (source lore):** A major scholarly complex in Al-Rutbah, formed from interconnected domed buildings, white-marble courts, mosaics, and gardens.
- **Control (proposal):** Chief Librarian Amineh Rafi heads an independent custodial council. Ruhania's crown funds part of the institution but does not personally own its collections.
- **Collections (source lore):** Astronomy and planar study, magic and alchemy, artistic archives, and a garden devoted to histories and philosophy. “Ilm Ki Dunya” remains the source heading associated with the Grand Codexium.
