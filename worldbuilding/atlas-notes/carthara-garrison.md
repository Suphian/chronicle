# Vael's Garrison

- **Area (current draft):** Carthara's military and guard complex, containing barracks, records offices, sentry posts, and detention facilities.
- **Control (current draft):** General Tarquin Vael, the Third Seat, commands the city guard, including Dyia.
- **Importance (current draft):** The center of the city's organized force, guard employment, custody, and official records. Its exact street plan and local map position remain unwritten.
