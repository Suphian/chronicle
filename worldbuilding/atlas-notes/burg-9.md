# Al-Rutbah

- **Area (source lore):** Ruhania's western capital in the Zulimar wetlands, with houses on stilts, boat landings, and an extensive network of waterways.
- **Control (proposal):** Governor Nahla Qadir administers the wetland province for Queen Samira Daran. Waterway councils manage local channels and flood works.
- **Landmarks (source lore):** The Grand Codexium occupies a major scholarly complex. The partly submerged palace gardens of Jannat Al-Rutbah lie in the surrounding wetland region.
