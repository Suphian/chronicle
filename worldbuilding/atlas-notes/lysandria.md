# Lysandria

- **Area (current draft):** An island east of the mainland with two enormous mountains, coastal settlements, pottery workshops, healers, and rare metals.
- **Control (current draft):** General Phylios rules the island. His household and military authority coexist with local communities whose formal governing powers remain to be developed.
- **People and places (current draft):** Amara's island home and Alethea's birthplace; Hanno spends years studying and working here. The nearby Moonlit Isle is a separate place.
