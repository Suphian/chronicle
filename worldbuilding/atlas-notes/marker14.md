# The Altar of Windswept Watchers

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **The cleaned inscription:** Caretakers brush sand from carved names while visitors debate how damaged passages should be read.
- **The small offering:** Families bring cloth, water, or the labor of repair as well as more elaborate gifts.
- **An imposed ceremony:** A prosperous patron wants the shrine opened around his schedule, displacing a quieter household's remembrance.
