# The Altar of Windswept Watchers

- **Area (source lore):** A sandstone shrine in the Catharan desert, carved with images of great Skywatcher birds and ancestral inscriptions, surrounded by standing pillars.
- **Control (proposal):** A hereditary order of shrine custodians manages the altar and nearby pilgrim shelters. The surrounding approaches fall under local Catharan civil authority.
- **Purpose (source lore):** A center of ancestor remembrance and offerings associated with the Ancestral Skywatchers; the faith does not establish that the birds literally contain ancestral spirits.
