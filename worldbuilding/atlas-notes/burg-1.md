# Aegis

- **Area (source lore):** Lysandria's capital beside Harmony Lagoon, built from local stone and timber along the water's edge.
- **Control (proposal):** A civic governor appointed by General Phylios manages the city; a harbor council regulates berths and landing places. Phylios's rule over Lysandria is established.
- **Landmarks (proposal):** A terraced citadel above the civic square, lagoon quays, healing courts, and a lower pottery quarter.
