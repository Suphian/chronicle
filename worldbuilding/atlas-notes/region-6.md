# Cathara

- **Area (source lore):** The old map's inland realm of desert, oases, underground trading passages, and greener hills around Elarion. Tijara is its oasis capital; Majlis Gate opens into the Souq Caverns.
- **Control (source lore):** Lord Numarius is described as a subordinate ruler under the Yáolán Empire and protector of Elarion. This older kingdom-wide role is not yet reconciled with the current Three Seats.
- **Current Carthara (current draft):** The coastal city is governed jointly by Numarius, Corvo, and Vael, who hold the fields, harbor, and garrison respectively. Its exact site within the wider map region remains open.
