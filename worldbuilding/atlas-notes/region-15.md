# Heretics

Status: **Proposed development · September 2026**. Invented map notes inspired by the author's geography and Mizan source material; these are possibilities for the world, not events established in the chapters.

- **A contested label:** “Heretics” could be a hostile mapmaker's category rather than the inhabitants' chosen name.
- **Common ovens:** Neighbors with incompatible beliefs share a baking court, disputing fuel contributions more often than doctrine.
- **The empty lectern:** A proposed open school lets teachers disagree before their pupils; its patrons are less enthusiastic when a lesson questions their privileges.

**Open continuity:** The source label does not establish a shared religion or endorse an accusation.
