# Heretics

- **Area (proposal):** A grassland confederation of fortified towns, open grazing country, and independent schools. Braurias is its proposed council seat.
- **Control (proposal):** A League of Free Houses governs through town delegates, with Speaker Maelin Rusk representing the league abroad. No single ruler owns the member towns.
- **Name (open question):** “Heretics” may be an accusation used by outsiders. The inhabitants' chosen name and the religious dispute behind the label remain undecided.
