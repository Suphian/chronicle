# The Silken Chain

ID: `the-silken-chain` · Members called Silkers · Existing draft network

## Established in current draft

Distributes Ravash in Carthara under powerful protection. The cure and Dyia's patrols break its visible operations, but its leadership is not discovered. Current author notes name Corvo as its secret head. Dyia kills some members without trial, including Amara's supplier.

Sources: [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), canon section; [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts); Chapters [Two](../../src/content/chapters/02-bitter-apprenticeship.ts), [Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), [Seven](../../src/content/chapters/07-rising-influence.ts).

## Interpretation

The name currently covers a whole distribution system. Sellers, carriers, organizers, and protected financiers need not know the same things or have equal freedom to leave. Recognizing those differences deepens the world without making the trade harmless.

## Proposed development

Identify how money and information move and where members depend on a legitimate trade. Give one lower-level participant a concrete reason to cooperate with an investigation and a realistic fear of doing so. Distinguish the surviving Chain from Hanno's new network; similarity does not establish identical membership.

## Open questions

Who produces Ravash? What survives after the cure? How does Corvo communicate without exposing herself? What did the murdered supplier know? A coerced member, a true believer, and a profiteer should not all share one stock motivation.
