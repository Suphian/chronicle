# The Last Standard

Working organization ID: `the-last-standard` · Leonin armed resistance rejecting surrender

## Established in the current draft

The author established factions willing to fight to the death. In [Chapter Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), a wounded Last Standard fighter acknowledges an attack on an armed convoy supplying the force occupying Leonin land. He refuses to pretend nobody resists in order to obtain sympathy. The faction claims the lost homeland as its objective and rejects surrender to Numarius's order.

## Interests and constraints

They seek to end occupation and dispossession through armed resistance. They depend on people whose needs continue when fighters leave: caregivers, workers, and displaced households. The claim to defend those people does not establish their consent to every risk. Willingness to die can be sincere while imposing costs on dependents who want to live.

## Acts, claims, and questions

The convoy attack is acknowledged in the scene; no tactics are described. An allegation concerning a driver's children remains unverified. Chuluun demands evidence instead of automatically defending it. The faction's command rules, accountability, treatment of captives, and boundaries on targets remain unwritten. “Illegal warfare” is not a substitute for specifying an act or whose law is invoked.

Propaganda's blanket terrorist label does not establish that every Leonin is a combatant. Its falsity also does not make every possible action by a fighter just. The [Open Hand](the-open-hand.md) disputes this faction's strategy; the [Return Councils](the-return-councils.md) must answer for civilians the fighters cannot simply claim to represent.
