# The Return Councils

Working organization ID: `the-return-councils` · Leonin camp representation

## Established in the current draft

The author established politically divided Leonin communities; this working name gives one strand a concrete form. In [Chapter Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), Chuluun helps councils keep household, missing-person, and labor-transfer records. He represents one shelter row's immediate needs rather than ruling every camp. Their lost-home flags also belong to Leonin who disagree with them.

## Interests and constraints

They want families located, coerced workers released, civilians represented, and claims to homes preserved. Their leverage is coordination: records, distributions, and the ability to show whom another body has failed to account for. Bombardment destroys both people and evidence. Compulsory work removes time from meetings; residents without a trusted representative can disappear from the process.

## Proposed development

Give a council a dispute over whether aid should follow household size, illness, or damage. Let a representative lose the confidence of the row that selected them. The authority to speak for a camp must be earned and challengeable, not granted by age alone.

## Open questions

How are delegates selected or removed? What can a council refuse when a fighter or labor office demands cooperation? Councils can negotiate immediate releases without sharing every position of the [Open Hand](the-open-hand.md); they can defend a return claim without endorsing every act of the [Last Standard](the-last-standard.md).
