# The Bakhtari

ID: `the-bakhtari` · Existing draft highland peoples

## Established in current draft

People of Bakhtar, described in the legend as cliff dwellers, flute players, and elven-blooded. The tale recounts invasions, life under threat, negotiations, and Iskandar's marriage to Rukhsana. One telling traces their line to Amara; its connection to her adopted Lysandrian origin remains unresolved. The tale does not establish that every Bakhtari community lived alike or that the people built no cities.

Sources: Chapter [Zero](../../src/content/chapters/00-the-conqueror.ts); [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts).

## Interpretation

Resistance is a narrated historical pattern, not proof of a single immutable identity. Highland communities must still negotiate food, kinship, routes, authority, and relationships with outsiders. The clan or communal forms governing these are not yet established.

## Proposed development

Develop disagreements between valleys over accepting tribute, marriage, migration, or trade. If the older ancestry claim is retained, establish its route to Lysandria without assuming Amara personally migrated from Bakhtar. Include people who remember ordinary peace and craft, not only invaders.

## Open questions

What constitutes belonging: residence, language, kinship, or several things? How do people interpret Rukhsana's marriage? Which customs changed after conquest? Neither elven blood nor mountain geography should serve as a total explanation of culture.
