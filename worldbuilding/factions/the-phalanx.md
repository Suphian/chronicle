# The Lysandrian Phalanx

ID: `the-phalanx` · Existing draft warrior society

## Established in current draft

Spear fighters who prize balance. Hanno trains among them for five years and is described in the codex as becoming the best spear in the region. Phylios presents Zaharaz for saving his son. The prologue recalls an earlier unsuccessful invasion of Bakhtar.

Sources: [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts); Chapters [Zero](../../src/content/chapters/00-the-conqueror.ts) and [Four](../../src/content/chapters/04-gift-of-lysandria.ts).

## Interpretation

Collective military discipline and an exceptional individual's dueling ability are different skills. Hanno's mastery should not imply that he can perform a formation's work alone or command it without learning command.

## Proposed development

Establish recruitment, length of service, the support economy, and a training peer who has strengths Hanno lacks. Decide whether the failure in Bakhtar is taught honestly, commemorated defensively, or contested. A doctrine of balance can justify restraint to one officer and militarized hierarchy to another.

## Open questions

Is this an army, elite order, civic duty, or school? Who may leave? Who pays for metals and food? Does “best spear” describe a contest, a reputation, or omniscient ranking? The early Sparta comparison is an influence, not a completed account of institutions.
