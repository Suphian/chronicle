# The Scattered Hearths

Working organization ID: `the-scattered-hearths` · Leonin diaspora support and testimony

## Established in the current draft

The author established displaced Leonin living throughout the world. In [Chapter Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), the Scattered Hearths send small payments from several cities, letters, offers to house relatives, and inquiries after detained family members. Every Leonin carries the lost home's flag; a traveling merchant keeps his inside a case. Life elsewhere does not erase the homeland or produce a single political position.

## Interests and constraints

They work toward reunion, housing, livelihoods, release from coercive claims, truthful accounts of the camps, and support for people unable to leave. Their resources come from ordinary work and relationships rather than one inexhaustible donor. Host-country restrictions, propaganda, competing family needs, and fear that names will expose relatives constrain what they can do.

## Proposed development

Give a household enough money either to sponsor one person's departure or fund several families through a season. Let someone building a new life resent being treated as a source of funds while still sending what they can. Return, safety elsewhere, and preserving a child's connection to home can be simultaneous desires.

## Open questions

How are funds entrusted and accounts challenged? Which communities remain under forced labor abroad? How do members verify testimony without making a perfect document the price of believing survivors? Relationships with the [Open Hand](the-open-hand.md), [Last Standard](the-last-standard.md), and [Return Councils](the-return-councils.md) vary; there is no single diaspora vote.
