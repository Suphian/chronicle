# The City Guard

ID: `the-city-guard` · Existing draft institution

## Established in current draft

Vael commands the guard. It keeps order and collects debts, including the seizure of the Averroes household. Dyia joins and rises to captain. He dismantles Silker operations but also bypasses trials and kills people. Numarius later presses for an end to the Dust crisis.

Sources: [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts); Chapters [One](../../src/content/chapters/01-market-awnings.ts), [Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), and [Nine](../../src/content/chapters/09-shadows-closing-in.ts).

## Interpretation

Its public service and coercion occupy the same chain of command. Dyia's desire to change the institution from inside requires actual mechanisms of influence; rank alone cannot make that credible.

## Proposed development

Define pay, recruitment, arrest records, hearings, and how orders from another Seat reach a captain. Give guards different backgrounds and reasons for serving. A reliable clerk or reluctant witness can make investigation more substantive than repeated violent interrogation.

## Open questions

Who hears complaints? What consequences follow refusal of an eviction detail? Are urban policing and military defense separate duties? Which of Dyia's superiors know his killings? Do not describe an institution as wholly corrupt while relying on its unexplained integrity to solve the plot.
