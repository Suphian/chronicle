# The Open Hand

Working organization ID: `the-open-hand` · Leonin advocates of negotiated peace

## Established in the current draft

The author's adopted political division includes Leonin who want peace with Lord Numarius. In [Chapter Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), an Open Hand member gathers names for negotiations over releases and peace. She challenges a fighter to explain how dead comrades will recover children from labor yards. Her own people endure the same displacement and carry the same lost-home flag.

## Interests and constraints

They seek an end to bombardment, release from enslavement, family reunion, movement, and a negotiated future with return made possible. Seeking negotiation does not establish consent to dispossession. They need an interlocutor able to deliver an agreement; Numarius can offer a meeting while withholding practical change. Other residents may regard repeated talks as cover for continued coercion.

## Proposed development

Stage a disagreement over an offer that releases named workers but excludes everyone registered elsewhere. A delegate's family might benefit without making acceptance obviously right or wrong. Preserve the difference between a tactical ceasefire, durable peace, and surrender.

## Open questions

Which terms are essential, and who can bind the negotiators to them? How can residents withdraw consent? Their leaders, funding, internal disagreements, and protection against coercion are not yet written. The [Return Councils](the-return-councils.md) need not endorse every compromise; the [Last Standard](the-last-standard.md) rejects their willingness to bargain.
