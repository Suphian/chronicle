# First-pass integration and reading checks

The ten numbered chapters now contain **27,983 words** of first-pass narrative, plus a **1,699-word** optional highland legend. This is material for developmental editing, not a claim that the epic is finished. Chapters Three and Four in particular may become longer sequences. The last action beyond Dyia arriving at the door remains unwritten.

## What this pass brings together

- Story, People, World, and Writing room are the four primary sections. The [overall story outline](../story/overall-outline.md) gives a single readable account of the current arc; chapter scene plans remain available beneath it in the writing room.
- Chapters are continuous illustrated prose. Two new hand-drawn plates accompany the market and apprenticeship. Cinematic mode and automatic chapter audio have been removed. Text resizing, passage links, reading-place return, and previous/next chapters remain.
- The latest author revisions are reflected in prose and supporting entries: Sinna; Hanno's limited combat ability; an ordinary staff named Zaharaz; Lysandria as Amara's two-mountain island home; and the Leonin's displacement, forced labor, factions, diaspora, flag, and relationship with Numarius's people.
- The atlas's northwestern terrain is now a separate island with two dominant peaks. All positions remain schematic. Artwork prompts and provenance remain in the repository.
- The teacher inspired by Omar Mukhtar, the proposed Surety Quarter, and Sinna's connection to Ibn Sina have their own notes with historical sources and explicit proposal/adoption labels.

## Validation

The production build and TypeScript checks pass. ESLint passes. The cross-content validation script checks unique IDs, chapter/place links, image and audio assets, individual character dossiers, illustration placements, and local notebook links.

Browser checks covered the four sections, Sinna search and dossier, the complete story list, the overall outline, Lysandria selection and the travel overlay, chapter illustrations, larger text, and a 390px phone viewport. No horizontal page overflow was observed on the checked pages. Contents collapse on phones. Chapter pages contain no active music controls or audio player; the media shelf uses manual native playback controls, with all cues initially paused. The normal browser viewport was restored afterward.

## What still needs editorial decisions

The full age/lifespan table, the relationship of Amara's older ancestry to Lysandria, the generations in the Leonin displacement, the Three Seats' legal powers, and the wider arc remain open. Sinna's proposed sabotage-and-failed-rescue motive has not replaced the current deliberate killing. Alethea's ultimate fate and the brothers' final confrontation deserve structural review before they are treated as inevitable.

The illustrations are an initial visual treatment, not a complete illustrated edition of every chapter. Original Google Docs remain preserved as historical sources; source names and superseded descriptions there have not been rewritten.
