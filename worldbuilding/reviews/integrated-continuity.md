## P2 — Observed contradiction: Hanno's spear proficiency

**Evidence:** Chapter Four, [`ancient-guardians`](../../src/content/chapters/04-gift-of-lysandria.ts), says that in his fifth year there are soldiers Hanno cannot touch and an injured woman who defeats him. The codex's [`the-phalanx`](../../src/content/codex.ts) entry still says he became the best spear in the region after those five years. The supporting [Phalanx profile](../factions/the-phalanx.md) cites that old claim.

**Consequence:** The reader receives incompatible assessments of the same training period when moving from story to reference material. The new prose deliberately gives Hanno limits; the codex should not erase them.

**Recommended repair:** Revise the supporting claim to skilled training with stronger peers, unless the author deliberately wants a later contest or reputation to explain the old superlative. Do not restore effortless superiority merely to preserve an older summary. Sent to the lead and both relevant owners.

## P2 — Missing knowledge bridge: when Hanno learns Adil died

**Evidence:** Chapter Three, [`collision-course`](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), supplies no news of Adil beyond another unanswered inquiry. The Chapter Five text available during review contains no report of his death. Chapter Seven, [`the-heart-of-carthara`](../../src/content/chapters/07-rising-influence.ts), already has Hanno think of Adil among the dead. Chapter Eight, [`fertilizer`](../../src/content/chapters/08-a-dark-turn.ts), supplies Numarius's explicit degrading account.

**Classification:** This is a missing event in Hanno's knowledge, **not** an observed resurrection or contradictory date of death. It is possible to fill it without altering the main chronology.

**Recommended repair:** The Chapter Five owner can supply a limited death report, keeping circumstances and grave uncertain; alternatively Chapter Seven should preserve uncertainty until the banquet. A report's source and limits matter more than a new exact date. Sent to the Chapter Five owner and Six–Eight owner for coordination.

## P2 — Knowledge certainty exceeds Hanno's access

**Evidence:** Chapter Eight, [`a-network-of-shadows`](../../src/content/chapters/08-a-dark-turn.ts), is labeled Hanno's viewpoint but states that the broker does not know who first made the Dust. The scene establishes what Hanno told him—he presented himself as financier—but no way for Hanno to establish the broker's private conclusions.

**Consequence:** Treating successful concealment as narrator fact can mistakenly fix the broker's knowledge for later investigation. This is more than a preference about narrative distance because the plot depends on incomplete information.

**Recommended repair:** State what Hanno has withheld or claimed, and leave the broker's suspicions unconfirmed unless a deliberate narrator shift is intended. Sent to the owning agent.

## P3 — Existing plausibility issue at Sinna's demonstration

Chapters One and Two establish Sinna as a technically careful teacher. Chapter Six gives him a convincing desire to astonish patrons, but the reviewed version does not yet show what convinces him that publicly taking an unfamiliar stolen preparation is acceptably safe. This is a motivation/setup gap, not an impossible act: a competent person can choose a bad risk. A misleading preliminary check, credible documentary claim, or visible pressure would explain the particular lapse. The lead already received this feedback in the earlier sample review.

The poison parcel also risks reaching someone other than Sinna. The narrative already identifies Hanno's deliberate intent; noticing and handling or accepting the wider risk would make the method's consequences clearer. This is an expansion recommendation, not evidence that an unmentioned third party actually dies.

## P3 — Map associations should distinguish destination and memory

Chapter Eight's `elysian-dust` action occurs in the Cartharan garden and workshop, but the existing scene tag points to `moonlit-isle`. Chapter One's `the-fields` similarly stages departure from the Cartharan market while pointing to the destination `numarius-fields`.

The actual book companion labels its list **Places in this chapter**, so these links are legitimate associations and **not literal statements that the character is physically there**. A clear atlas link label such as “Wedding remembered” or “Adil is taken” preserves that distinction. Moving the Dust scene's primary tag to Carthara is also possible if the remembered island remains reachable. An initial stronger concern was corrected after inspecting the reader.
