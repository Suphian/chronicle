# Harmony Lagoon

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Nestled on the edge of Aegis, the capital of Lysandria, Harmony Lagoon is a tranquil body of water that mirrors the sky's azure expanse. The lagoon is a picturesque haven, its crystal-clear waters gently caressing the golden sands, reflecting the harmony between the island's natural beauty and its inhabitants.

According to Lysandrian folklore, the lagoon was formed when a beautiful celestial nymph, charmed by the island's splendor, decided to descend from the heavens. She chose a spot near the burgeoning settlement of Aegis and struck the earth, her celestial magic creating a beautiful lagoon that mirrored her heavenly home. The nymph, enchanted by the lagoon's tranquility, decided to make it her earthly abode, bringing with her a sense of peace and harmony that the lagoon continues to exude.

Over time, the lagoon has become a centerpiece of Lysandrian life. The gentle lapping of its waters sets a calming rhythm for the bustling city, and its serene beauty serves as a constant reminder of the island's harmonious ethos. It's not uncommon to see locals and visitors alike basking in the lagoon's peaceful ambiance, whether they're enjoying a quiet moment of solitude, engaging in peaceful contemplation, or partaking in friendly conversation.

Harmony Lagoon is more than just a body of water; it is a symbol of the island's peaceful spirit and harmony with nature. Its tranquil waters and peaceful ambiance make it a cherished part of Lysandria, reflecting the island's serene landscapes and the peaceful coexistence of its inhabitants with their natural surroundings.
