# Lustria the Capital of Volupta—The Isle of Delights

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Nestled in the heart of the ocean, Volupta—The Isle of Delights—is a sanctuary of joy and indulgence. A canvas of verdant greenery, vibrant blooms, and pristine beaches, this island delights every sense. The air, perfumed with exotic flora, echoes with laughter and music, creating an atmosphere of perpetual celebration.

At Volupta's heart is the Pavilion of Pleasures, a testament to the island's dedication to enjoyment. Offering everything from enchanting performances to luxurious baths, the Pavilion is a symbol of the island's commitment to pleasure.

The Voluptans, as the inhabitants are known, revel in life's delights. Their culture, steeped in the pursuit of pleasure, encourages living in the moment and embracing joy. Volupta, therefore, is a paradise for hedonists—a haven of constant celebration and indulgence.
