Barsbold Khulan

Tsagaan-Amgalan Tribe

The tribe from the Khulan Dynasty, known for their strong and imposing figures with wider heads, is led by a brave and fierce warrior named Barsbold Khulan.

Their tribe, the Tsagaan-Amgalan Tribe, is situated just north of the capital, Erdene Zuu, and is currently the only tribe completely surrounded by one of the warring superpowers. Despite the military occupation in their lands, the Tsagaan-Amgalan Tribe remains a bastion of fierce resistance, refusing to yield to the invaders.

Barsbold is a Mongolian name that means "firm hero" or "strong warrior," reflecting his unyielding resolve and courage in the face of adversity. The Tsagaan-Amgalan Tribe's name is derived from the Mongolian words "tsagaan," meaning white, and "amgalan," meaning peace or tranquility. The name symbolizes their desire for peace and freedom in their homeland, as well as their resilience and determination to protect their way of life against the encroaching superpower.
