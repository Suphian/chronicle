# Majlis Gate to Souq Caverns

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

The Souq Caverns, the labyrinthine network of tunnels beneath Cathara, serve as a bustling hub of commerce, a testament to the ingenuity of the Catharan people. However, the caverns' grand entrance, known as the Majlis Gate, is steeped in legend, adding to the allure of these subterranean streets.

Legend has it that when Tijara was still a young city, the people faced a severe problem. The relentless heat of the desert made daily activities arduous, and the city was in danger of losing its life and vibrancy. A young and ambitious architect named Idris, known for his inventive solutions, proposed an idea to King Malik Al Asad - to take the city underground during the day, using the natural caves and tunnels that honeycombed the area beneath the city.

The king, intrigued by this audacious plan, gave Idris his blessing. So began the herculean task of carving out the Souq Caverns. The most challenging part was the creation of the Majlis Gate - a grand entrance to the caverns that would not only serve as a functional point of access but also a symbol of Tijara's resilience and adaptability.

Idris, according to the tales, sought the aid of the very same djinn who had once helped King Malik Al Asad repel the invaders at Al Qal'a. The djinn, impressed by Idris's courage and vision, agreed to help. With a wave of his hand, the djinn created a majestic gateway out of the solid rock, complete with intricate carvings that told the story of Tijara's birth and the Bahar Oasis's creation.

The Majlis Gate, which means "The Gathering Gate" in the old Catharan language, stands as a towering masterpiece of architecture and a beacon of Catharan resilience. Its intricate carvings serve as a constant reminder of the city's rich history and the sacrifices made by its people. The gate leads to the bustling Souq Caverns, where the life of the city continues unabated, shielded from the harsh desert sun.

The Souq Caverns and the Majlis Gate, thus, not only play a crucial role in the daily life of the Catharan people but are also integral parts of the city's lore, embodying the spirit of innovation, adaptability, and resilience that characterizes the people of Cathara.
