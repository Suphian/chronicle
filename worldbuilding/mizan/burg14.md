# Titanus the Capital of Tharsis—The Isle of Strength

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Tharsis—The Isle of Strength—is a rugged land of towering cliffs and rocky expanses, named after the mythical titan of endurance. This harsh landscape is home to a society of warriors and hunters, their strength and resilience honed by the island's demanding terrain.

At the heart of Tharsis stands the Coliseum of Titans, a massive stone structure where the island's inhabitants test their mettle in combat and strategy. The annual Tournament of Titans, held within its formidable walls, is a grand spectacle of power and skill, drawing spectators from across the islands, including Lysandria.

The inhabitants of Tharsis, known as Tharsians, are revered for their physical prowess and their indomitable spirit. The island's culture venerates strength and endurance, making Tharsis a bastion of power amidst the ocean's expanse.
