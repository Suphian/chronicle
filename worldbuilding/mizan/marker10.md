# Chuluun

Backstory

Chuluun is a classic antihero, often humorous without intending or even realizing it. Born as the prince of the Altan Tribe, his mother held the prestigious title of Arban Terguun, the Chief Speaker, while his father served as the Chief Commander of the tribe's armed forces. Growing up, Chuluun wanted for nothing. He was educated in multiple languages, possessed wisdom beyond his years, and excelled in Leonin combat, mastering various martial arts and weapons.

Yet despite his privileged upbringing, Chuluun yearned for a simple nomadic life. This desire put him at odds with the expectations of his tribe, who envisioned a bright future for their prince. As Chuluun reached young adulthood, the idea of exploring the world consumed him entirely.

When the day of his arranged marriage to Sarnai (Сарнай) - Rose, a symbol of beauty and strength - a Leonin princess from the neighboring powerful clan, the Tsetseg (Цэцэг) - Flower Tribe, arrived, Chuluun made a fateful decision. He packed a few of his most prized possessions: his trusty Khar-Tugai (a traditional Leonin weapon), a pouch of dried fish from nearby rivers (his favorite snack), a weathered copy of the philosophical tome "Whispers of the Wind," and a beautiful piece of armor adorned with his family's crest - a roaring Leonin encircled by a golden chain, symbolizing Chuluun's internal struggle between his wanderlust and duty to his tribe.

With his belongings in tow, Chuluun embarked on a journey of self-discovery and adventure. He wandered through vast deserts, scaled towering mountains, and delved into the deepest caverns. Along the way, he encountered myriad creatures, both friend and foe, and honed his skills as a Blood Hunter. His wanderlust seemingly insatiable, he continued to explore the world around him.

However, as the years passed, Chuluun's newfound wisdom began to manifest as regret. He realized the importance of his people's way of life and the responsibility he had to preserve it. This epiphany led Chuluun to question his choices and ponder the path that lay before him.

Now, at the age of 41, Chuluun finds himself at a crossroads. Will he continue his journey as a wandering Blood Hunter, or will he return to his tribe and embrace the destiny that awaits him as the prince of the Altan Tribe? The winds of fate swirl around Chuluun, waiting for him to make a choice that will determine not only his own future but the future of his people as well.

How he became a Blood Hunter:

As Chuluun was traveling through a dense forest, he was suddenly ambushed by a group of Dark Fairies. These twisted creatures were known to capture and enslave young Leonin from the wild, using dark magic to force them into their slave army. The Dark Fairies were seeking a rare resource that could only be found in the depths of the forest, and they needed the strength and race abilities of the Leonin to help them locate it.

Chuluun was captured along with several other Leonin, and he found himself imprisoned in the dark dungeons of the fairy kingdom. The conditions were harsh, and the other Leonin were forced to labor day and night to help the Dark Fairies amass their precious resource.

One night, as Chuluun lay in his cell, he was visited by the spirit of his banished grandfather. The old Leonin appeared before him, urging him to strike a bargain with an otherworldly being known as The Hexblade. The Hexblade was a powerful entity that could imbue a weapon with dark magic, granting its wielder incredible strength and abilities.

Though hesitant at first, Chuluun eventually agreed to the deal, knowing that it was the only way to save himself and his fellow captives. With the Hexblade's power, Chuluun was able to break free from his cell and fight his way out of the Dark Fairy kingdom.

After his escape, Chuluun discovered that the rare resource the Dark Fairies were seeking was actually the essence of moonlight, which they used to fuel their dark magic. The Hexblade had warned him that the power he had gained came at a price, and that he would forever be hunted by the Dark Fairies for betraying them.

From that day forward, Chuluun became a Blood Hunter, using his newfound powers and skills to hunt down those who would harm innocents and protect his people from all manner of supernatural threats.
