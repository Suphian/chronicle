# Ilm Ki Dunya

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

The Grand Codexium of Al-Rutbah is a magnificent and unparalleled center of knowledge located in the heart of the city. As the largest library in the world, it houses an extensive collection of books, scrolls, and artifacts from various cultures and civilizations. It's grand architecture and awe-inspiring design reflects the importance placed on learning and the pursuit of wisdom by the Ruhaniyya people.

Structure:

The library is a sprawling complex of interconnected buildings, each dedicated to a specific field of knowledge. It features towering, domed structures made of white marble and adorned with intricate mosaics depicting scenes of scholarly pursuit and the sharing of wisdom. The Grand Codexium's courtyards and gardens are filled with lush greenery, providing tranquil spaces for reading, contemplation, and discussion.

Collections:

The Grand Codexium's vast collection spans diverse fields of knowledge, including history, science, philosophy, magic, art, and religion. In addition to written works, the library houses numerous artifacts, such as maps, scientific instruments, and magical relics. The library's dedicated curators work tirelessly to acquire new materials, ensuring that the Grand Codexium remains at the forefront of intellectual discovery.
