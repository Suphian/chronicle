# Hikari no Kuni

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Hikari no Kuni (Land of Light) is a kingdom nestled in the northeastern reaches, a realm characterized by its humble elegance, a stark contrast to the vibrant diversity of Sidrat Al Muntaha. It is a land inhabited by elf-like beings, known as the Hikarujin, who follow a cultural ethos strongly influenced by the aesthetics and principles akin to Japanese traditions. Their lives are guided by a simple yet profound adage: "Seek the Light within and without."

Religion permeates all aspects of life in Hikari no Kuni. The Hikarujin share the same monotheistic faith as the people of Sidrat Al Muntaha, worshipping a deity embodying Light. This entity, known as Hikari-no-Kami, is believed to have bestowed the gift of life and Light upon the world. The Hikarujin believe in the divine power of Light as a force of wisdom, purity, and guidance. Their spiritual practices involve rituals of meditation and prayer, aimed at invoking the Light's blessings and cultivating inner illumination.

Despite the vastness of their kingdom, Hikari no Kuni is home to only one holy site: a solitary mountain named 'Kōmyōsan' or 'Mountain of Radiance'. While not as grand as the mountains of Sidrat Al Muntaha, Kōmyōsan holds immense significance for the Hikarujin. They believe the mountain is a conduit for Hikari-no-Kami's divine energy. Many devotees embark on spiritual pilgrimages to the mountain, seeking enlightenment and a deeper connection with their deity.

The Hikarujin live humble, minimalist lives, placing great value on introspection, discipline, and the pursuit of spiritual perfection. This ethos is particularly evident in the lives of the Hikarujin monks, who devote themselves entirely to spiritual practice, often living in isolated monasteries scattered across the kingdom. These monks are revered as wise sages and spiritual guides, their teachings forming the bedrock of Hikari no Kuni's societal values.

The Paladins of Hikari no Kuni are akin to samurais, embodying a perfect blend of spiritual strength and martial prowess. Known as the Hikari Kenshi (Warriors of Light), these elite warriors adhere to a strict code of honor, similar to the samurai's bushido. They are expertly trained in the use of the 'Hikari Katana', swords infused with divine Light that symbolizes their commitment to uphold justice and righteousness. The Hikari Kenshi serve as the kingdom's guardians and protectors, vowing to use their divine gifts to safeguard their land and people from any harm. Their steadfast dedication and unwavering loyalty have earned them deep respect and admiration across Hikari no Kuni.
