# Arban Naran of the Uulyn-Khökh Tribe

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Arban Naran

Uulyn-Khökh Tribe

The tribe far northeast of the capital, surrounded by both warring and peaceful nations, belongs to the Erdeni Dynasty. They are ruled by a Leonin leader who appears far more human than lion, named Arban Naran. His name reflects his more human-like appearance, with "Arban" meaning "ten" in Mongolian and "Naran" meaning "sun," symbolizing a unique and radiant presence.

Arban Naran leads the Uulyn-KhökhTribe, . They are strategically fortified atop a high mountain peak, neighboring an extremely robust trading center known as the Majlis Gate. This area is rich with tunnel networks that traverse the mountainous region, providing the tribe with valuable trading opportunities and a strong defensive position.

Tribe: Uulyn-Khökh Tribe - "Mountain's Crown" (derived from the Mongolian words "uul," meaning mountain, and "khökh," meaning crown or top, signifying the tribe's location at the peak of a high mountain)

While the Uulyn-Khökh Tribe may not be as directly involved in the conflict as some of the other tribes, they use their intelligence and ability to blend in with the crowd to maintain a noble, low-intensity resistance movement. They focus on more subversive operations, leveraging their strategic position and connections to the Majis Gate to subtly undermine their enemies and support their fellow Leonin tribes in the struggle.
