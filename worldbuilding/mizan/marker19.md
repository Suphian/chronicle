# Commander Boldsaikhan (meaning "steel axe")

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Five generations ago, the five Mergen Dynasty brothers tactfully retreated into the vast neighboring kingdom of Zorig, a tropical rainforest akin to the Congo. After years of fierce resistance and feeling abandoned by their fellow Leonin tribes in the south, the Mergen brothers sought refuge in Zorig. With permission from the Monkey King of Zorig, named Jirgal (meaning "fortune"), four of the five Mergen brothers resettled in the northwestern part of his kingdom. In exchange for protection, they became vassals of King Jirgal's realm.

Batmunkh Mergen, however, made a different deal with King Jirgal. By agreeing to marry the king's daughter, Princess Lhagvaa (meaning "light"), Batmunkh Mergen was allowed to continue fighting his sworn eternal enemy and remain in his homeland, the mountain peak region of the Tsast-Urmai Tribe called Tengeri Uul (meaning "Sky Mountain").

Now, five generations later, nine of Batmunkh Mergen's descendants lead the resistance in the north using masterful guerrilla warfare tactics. They bide their time, waiting for the day they can reunite with their Mergen brethren. The nine descendants of Batmunkh Mergen are:

Altangerel (meaning "golden light")

Gerelchuluun (meaning "light of wisdom")

Erkhemsaikhan (meaning "mighty and wise")

Orgilmaa (meaning "sovereign beauty")

Oyuunchimeg (meaning "wisdom ornament")

Togtokh (meaning "enduring")

Enkhjargal (meaning "peace and happiness")

Munkhbat (meaning "eternal firmness")

Boldsaikhan (meaning "steel axe")

Among these nine descendants, Boldsaikhan stands out as the leader. He is the most savage and cruel of them all. One unique characteristic defining Boldsaikhan is a prominent scar across his face, which he received in battle against his enemies. The scar serves as a constant reminder of his unyielding determination to reclaim the lost legacy of the Mergen Dynasty and reestablish their rightful place among the Leonin tribes.
