# Alethea

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Alethea's beauty was striking yet subtle, an ethereal blend of grace and intellect. Her eyes, a deep shade of emerald green, sparkled with curiosity and kindness, framed by long, dark lashes that cast delicate shadows on her high cheekbones. Her full lips curved into a gentle smile that could warm even the coldest hearts, hinting at her compassionate nature and unwavering empathy.

Her long, wavy hair cascaded down her back, a rich, chestnut brown hue that shimmered under the sun's rays. The strands were adorned with delicate braids entwined with vibrant wildflowers, a tribute to the island's breathtaking flora. A silver circlet, inlaid with intricate designs inspired by the Isle of Wisdom, graced her forehead, accentuating her regal bearing.

Alethea's delicate features were enhanced by the soft glow of her sun-kissed skin, which held a natural radiance that seemed to emanate from within. Her slender yet strong frame was a testament to her dedication to both her intellectual pursuits and her physical well-being, embodying the harmony she sought in all aspects of life.

Intricate tattoos, inspired by the flora and fauna of Lysandria, adorned her arms and shoulders, weaving an enchanting tapestry of nature's beauty on her skin. Each tattoo held a special meaning for Alethea, representing the lessons she had learned, the friendships she had forged, and the discoveries she had made on her journey. Together, these intricate designs told the story of Alethea's quest, immortalizing her experiences in a living work of art.
