# Lord Numarius

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Lord Numarius, the half-elf puppet king of Cathara, was indeed a tyrant. His rule was marked by ruthless cunning and a willingness to do anything necessary to secure and maintain his power. From his earliest days, he had been born into a complex web of royal intrigue, a competitive nest of siblings all vying for the throne of Cathara. Yet, he proved the most ruthless and cunning of them all, using a combination of political machinations and cold-blooded murder to outmaneuver and eliminate his competitors.

His mother, Lady Viviana, was the last living descendant of the legendary Queen Al Zahrawas, and it was from her that Numarius learned the importance of patience and the long view in politics. His mixed heritage, tracing its roots back to Queen Al Zahrawas, added an aura of authenticity to his claim for the throne, even as his actions belied any noble intentions.

Cathara, once a beacon of peace and prosperity under the rule of Numarius's great-grandmother, Queen Belisara, was in a state of decline. The Yáolán Empire from the north had been steadily encroaching on their lands, annexing territories and seizing taverns. Cathara, lacking military power, was on the brink of being completely overrun.

In these desperate times, Numarius made a deal with the devil, so to speak. The Yáolán Empire approached him with a proposition - they would make him the king of Cathara, in exchange for his loyalty and fealty. Seeing an opportunity, Numarius orchestrated a coup against Queen Belisara, unseating her and taking the throne for himself.

Yet, his newfound power came at a cost. As a puppet king, his kingdom was essentially a vassal state of the Yáolán Empire. His rule was one of oppression and fear, as he used the might of the Yáolán Empire to enforce his will and suppress any opposition. He became a tyrant, his name synonymous with betrayal and cruelty.

Despite his oppressive rule, Numarius harbored a glimmer of hope. He aspired to one day free Cathara from the Yáolán Empire's control and return it to its former glory. It was a dream that seemed distant and unreachable, but it was one that Numarius clung to, even as he sat on a throne that was not truly his.
