# Tengeri Uul

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

Tengeri Uul, the majestic mountain peak homeland of the Tsast-Urmai Tribe, is a formidable and awe-inspiring natural fortress in the northern reaches of the Tengeri Wastes. The name "Tengeri Uul" translates to "Sky Mountain," a fitting description for a peak that seems to touch the heavens themselves. This remote and inhospitable region has long served as a natural citadel for the Tsast-Urmai Tribe, providing them with a defensible position against enemies and invaders.

The landscape surrounding Tengeri Uul is characterized by treacherous cliffs, deep ravines, and narrow passages, making it nearly impossible for large armies to approach the Tsast-Urmai stronghold. The terrain demands a certain level of agility and skill to navigate, which the Leonin of the Tsast-Urmai Tribe have honed over generations. This has led the tribe to become known for their exceptional mountaineering and guerrilla warfare abilities.

The Tsast-Urmai Tribe has a deep spiritual connection to Tengeri Uul, believing that the peak is the home of their ancestral spirits and a conduit to the divine realm. As such, they have established numerous sacred sites throughout the mountain range, including hidden shrines, meditation caves, and burial grounds. These sites are fiercely protected and revered by the Tsast-Urmai Tribe, who view them as a source of spiritual guidance and strength.

At the heart of Tengeri Uul lies the ancestral stronghold of the Tsast-Urmai Tribe, a fortified city carved directly into the mountain itself. The city, known as Urmai-Khüree, is a testament to the tribe's architectural prowess and their ability to adapt to the harsh environment. The labyrinthine network of tunnels, chambers, and terraces provide ample living space, storage, and defense for the tribe's inhabitants. In times of war, the stronghold is nearly impregnable, with hidden passages and secret escape routes ensuring the tribe's survival.

Despite the inhospitable nature of Tengeri Uul, the Tsast-Urmai Tribe has managed to thrive in this environment. They have adapted to the limited resources available, cultivating mountain herbs, raising hardy livestock, and developing ingenious methods to collect and store water. Trade with neighboring tribes and the bustling trading center at the Majis Gate has also helped to sustain the Tsast-Urmai Tribe, providing them with access to goods and resources from afar.

In recent times, the Tsast-Urmai Tribe has faced mounting pressure from the encroaching war between the two global superpowers. While the tribe's ancestral home at Tengeri Uul has provided a measure of protection, the Tsast-Urmai people have been forced to adapt and innovate to survive the changing world around them. Their cunning, resilience, and unwavering determination to preserve their way of life have only served to further cement the tribe's reputation as formidable warriors and tenacious survivors.
