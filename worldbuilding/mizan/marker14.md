# The Altar of Windswept Watchers

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

The Altar of Windswept Watchers is a revered shrine located in the heart of the vast Catharan desert. Rising like a mirage from the golden sands, this sacred structure serves as a testament to the people's deep respect for the Ancestral Skywatchers and their unwavering faith in the guidance and protection of their forebears.

Constructed from sun-bleached sandstone, the altar is intricately carved with depictions of the majestic Skywatchers soaring above the desert landscape, their vast wings outstretched as they keep a watchful eye on their descendants below. Etched alongside these images are ancient Catharan script and symbols, telling the stories of the ancestors who once walked the land and whose spirits now reside within the Skywatchers.

Surrounding the altar, a circle of tall, slender obelisks stands sentinel, each adorned with vibrant banners that flutter gently in the desert breeze, creating an ethereal melody that whispers of the ancestors' wisdom. The base of each obelisk is embedded with precious stones and metals, offerings left by generations of Catharans in the hope of receiving the Skywatchers' blessings.

At the center of the shrine, a raised platform holds a polished stone pedestal upon which offerings of food, incense, and other tributes are placed during religious ceremonies. It is here that the people of Cathara gather to honor their ancestors, seeking guidance and protection as they navigate the challenges of life.

The Altar of Windswept Watchers stands as a beacon of hope and unity amidst the unforgiving desert landscape, a reminder to the people of Cathara that they are never truly alone, and that the spirits of their ancestors continue to watch over them from the boundless skies above.
