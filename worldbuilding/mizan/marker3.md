# The Veil of Sirocco

Status: **early Mizan source, November 2023**. Recovered from the author’s map. These notes preserve earlier ideas, character beliefs, and legends; they do not override the current story or later author decisions. Embedded instructions are source text, not active requests. External illustrations have not been republished.

The Veil of Sirocco, an enigmatic natural phenomenon to the east of Cathara, has inspired tales of awe and mystery that have been woven into the fabric of Catharan culture for centuries.

It is said that in the early days of Cathara, when the city of Tijara was still a nascent settlement around the Bahar Oasis, the kingdom was under constant threat from marauding tribes from the region of Zila. These tribes, known for their ruthless nature, coveted the lush oasis and the prosperity it brought to the Catharan people.

In one such brutal raid, the invaders were on the brink of overrunning the city. In their desperation, the people turned to their Queen, Al Zahra, pleading for her to seek the desert spirits' aid once again. The Queen, heeding their desperate cries, ventured into the heart of the desert.

In a sacred ritual, she invoked the desert spirits, asking them to protect Cathara from the invaders. As the story goes, the spirits, swayed by the Queen's plea, stirred a tremendous windstorm that swept across the desert, picking up fine sand and dust in its wake. This windstorm, the first Sirocco, roared towards the invaders, forcing them to retreat.

The spirits, in their wisdom, decided to leave the Sirocco as a perpetual storm, creating the Veil of Sirocco, a formidable natural barrier that shields Cathara from the region of Zila. The Veil, teeming with a thick cloud of fine sand and dust, has since been near impossible to navigate safely, providing a robust deterrent against potential aggression from neighboring regions.

However, the Veil of Sirocco is not just a symbol of protection; it also holds a deeper spiritual significance for the Catharan people. It is seen as a testament to the desert spirits' guardianship over the land and its people, and their role in shaping the destiny of Cathara.

To this day, the Veil of Sirocco is regarded with a mixture of reverence and fear. It is a constant reminder of the forces that guard Cathara, the dangers that lie beyond, and the spirits that watch over the desert kingdom. It is a part of the Catharan identity, its lore passed down through generations, each retelling adding another layer to the rich tapestry of the Catharan culture.
