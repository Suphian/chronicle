# Geography reconciliation

## Adopted Mizan map — September 2026

The author supplied the November 2023 Mizan map as the whole-world atlas and explicitly chose its old Leonin region for Tengeri. **Tengeri is now anchored southwest of Cathara/Carthara**, superseding the earlier far-eastern placement. Refugee camps, displacement, forced labor, bombardment, civilian lives, and diaspora remain current continuity; the source's freely roaming homeland portrayal does not return.

The imported atlas includes source coastlines, islands, region shapes, elevations, model climates, biomes, named places and written notes. The original planar scale is 5.11 miles per map unit. See the [source and reconstruction record](sources/maps/README.md). The older tables below describe unresolved lore relationships; references to an absent or schematic map are superseded where the imported source now supplies a position.

Lysandria has an identified island position east of the mainland. Its two enormous mountains and Amara's home remain adopted; individual mountain names, surveyed summits and town layouts remain open. Sidrat uses its source region. The old map's **Cathara is a region with inland capital Tijara**, whereas current **Carthara is a coastal city**. The regional anchor preserves both records without placing the current harbor inland. Carthara's districts, exact camp sites, Bakhtar and Moonlit Isle still need precise local placement. Person markers remain early source markers, not proof of current residence.

Right-click measurements use source scale. Walking, horse, boat and driving figures are editable planning comparisons, not canonical journey durations or proof that modern driving technology exists. They do not route around mountains or shorelines. The author prioritized importing and exploring content first; shared team editing is deferred.

## Latest adopted geography — Lysandria

The author clarified that **Lysandria is an island with two enormous mountains, and Amara comes from there**. The immediately preceding name “Jira” was corrected to Lysandria; it does not establish another place. Keep the existing `lysandria` ID and links. The decision preserves Phylios's rule, the local spear-training tradition, rare metals, and Alethea's Lysandrian origin. It establishes no kinship between Amara and Alethea.

This supersedes any claim that Amara's place of origin was Bakhtar. Earlier Bakhtari ancestry and the Rukhsana–Iskandar legend remain recorded draft material whose relationship to Amara's Lysandrian upbringing is unresolved. Do not invent migration generations, a shared coastline, or Lysandria's inclusion in Bakhtar to resolve the difference. The preserved source documents remain unchanged.

The Mizan source now supplies the island's position and extent. Individual mountain names and summit heights, harbor locations, and navigable sea-route distances remain open. [Chapter Three](../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts) now brings Hanno to Lysandria by sea: he sees the two mountains from the vessel, recalls Amara's pottery-bench memory, lands, and joins carts on the road above the harbor. There is no established road all the way from Sidrat. Moonlit Isle remains a separate island near Lysandria's coast. See the [place profile](places/lysandria.md) and [Amara's profile](characters/amara.md) for adopted facts, limited new draft memories, and proposed personal ties.

Three named mountains add religious and military places: **Jabal Al-Nur**, described as the highest of the three with a summit temple and revelation tradition; **Jabal Al-Sirr**, associated with caves and lost scholarship; and **Jabal Al-Hifaz**, a fortified barrier against an unnamed hostile kingdom to the west. Glowblossom Trees, Lumine Vines, and Whispering Reeds are recovered flora, with no cultivation or commercial rules yet established.

## Reconcile before promoting into the map

**Chuluun's status:** The narrative and current codex call him an elder; geography explicitly calls him prince of the Altan Tribe with a potential role in restoring the Khanate. “Prince” and “elder” need not be mutually exclusive, but the age, title, chronology, and personal arc are unestablished. Options for an author decision are: one person with both roles; different periods of one life; or an explicit revision favoring one depiction. Creating a second Chuluun or a relative is not supported by the sources. Do not make him Khan as a consequence of recovery alone.

**Religious relationships:** Solunari is named, dual-deity worship; Sidrat's deity is not named here; Hanno's Aetherium Harmonia comes from the questionnaire. Shared vocabulary of balance is insufficient evidence of common origins or theology. Keep separate entries and allow eventual relationships through researched, proposed exchange, disagreement, or coexistence. The requested Islamic history influence should be developed on its own terms; Solunari pilgrimage is not automatically Hajj, and a sun-and-moon temple is not automatically an Islamic institution.

**Continents and travel:** The source gives Ruhania's internal cardinal relations, not a world map. Retain existing map positions as schematic while adding a source-derived regional diagram only if labeled as such. First settle how Ruhania connects to the existing regions, whether the Azure Sea connects to Carthara, and where Astral Isle sits. Then choose scale, routes, seasons, and travel times. Preserve uncertainty rather than drawing a confident invented coastline.

**Sanctuary and danger:** Sidrat promises travel free from attack by people or wildlife, plentiful provisions, and generous hosts. The same document gives a treacherous route to Amal and a heavily defended frontier. Possible reconciliations include a maintained sanctuary network with limits, a religious ideal rather than universal fact, or explicit magic with defined scope and cost. None is chosen. A hostile neighbor does not prove sanctuary impossible, but it requires enforcement and boundaries.

**Climate and water:** Jahnavi divides desert and wetlands, but a river alone does not explain such a climate boundary. Establish the watershed, seasonal flooding, topography, irrigation, water rights, and delta before using it as an economic engine. This does not require replacing the author's desert/wetland contrast. It requires enough physical or magical support for travel, farming, and conflict to be consistent.

**Leonin subsistence:** Source language makes sparse vegetation the only food source yet also depicts hunting, wildlife movement, and communal meals. Clarify ordinary diets, water access, herd or game ecology, imports, storage, and seasonal mobility. Do not infer a population capable of large armies from repeated praise of resilience. Shared longing for reunification need not erase tribal councils or those who value autonomy.

**Knowledge infrastructure:** A world-scale library in a wetland city needs preservation, funding, copying, access, labor, and authority over acquisition. These are productive questions, not established defects. “Secure” magical holdings immediately create an access question, but neither censorship nor conspiracy is yet canon. Allow scholars to have incompatible but defensible obligations.

## Proposed integration order

1. **Recover visibly:** Link the preserved source and this ledger from the planning interface. Label new places “recovered source; placement unresolved” until mapped. Do not imply all details are already agreed canon.
2. **Resolve identities and relations:** Decide Chuluun's status, the three religious contexts, Ruhania's relationship to the current map, and the greater sovereign question. These choices materially affect profiles and routes.
3. **Build regional maps:** Expand Sidrat around its existing anchor, map Ruhania from its own river and capitals, and add an internal Tengeri diagram. Do not use an attractive image as evidence for new borders.
4. **Develop independent character files:** Chuluun has an existing named source basis. Propose other regional viewpoint characters only with explicit draft status and objectives that survive without Hanno. No new protagonist is established here.
5. **Intertwine through consequences:** Use the [expandable arc structure](story-options.md) to test knowledge exchange, water and transport obligations, Leonin autonomy, and sanctuary policy. The author has now adopted Numarius's people's connection to Leonin hospitality and subsequent dispossession; Chapter Three introduces it. Links to Dust or the historical maritime atrocity remain proposals. The original fragmented Khanate and prince biography require reconciliation with the later refugee-camp premise rather than automatic restoration.

The most useful next integration is to expand **Sidrat and the Leonin** from already shared source anchors while preserving **Ruhania as a substantial recovered region awaiting placement**. This avoids losing the author's early work and gives the epic independent centers of power without pretending the incomplete world map is settled.
