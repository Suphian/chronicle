# Areas to think about

These are geography and government decisions for the outline, gathered from the revised map descriptions at the author's request. **Every new area, ruler, office, holding, settlement layout, and economic role introduced in this pass is listed below as a proposal.** They are not adopted chapter events. Earlier source claims remain evidence to reconcile, not automatic present-day canon.

[Return to the overall outline](overall-outline.md#areas-to-think-about).

## Decisions to tackle first

- **Carthara and Cathara:** preserve the established Three Seats in the coastal city while deciding how inland Tijara, Elarion, and the older Yáolán overlordship relate to it. A ruler's office, a private estate, and military occupation are different forms of control.
- **Ruhania and Sidrat:** consider the proposed crowns and district administrations below against the author's existing regional geography. No new monarch is related to Hanno's family by this proposal.
- **Zorig and the Leonin:** consider Jirgal's government and the four proposed Mergen district seats without undoing present Leonin displacement, making Chuluun a universal ruler, or fixing the unresolved exile generations.
- **Lysandria and its neighbors:** preserve Phylios's established rule; decide whether the older Tharsis, Sophia, and Volupta settings are separate islands, dependencies, or source variants before fixing their allegiance.
- **Unfinished map regions:** the Fairy, Heretics, V B, and V W labels still need adopted names and identities. Their proposed governments below do not settle those questions.
