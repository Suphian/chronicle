The [researched candidate](ideas/the-teacher-of-the-uplands.md) distinguishes the real teacher and collective anti-colonial struggle from unsupported claims of one man nearly destroying an empire. The spoken Chitun/Chelune/Tungari references are interpreted as the established Chuluun/Tengeri names, not an explicit renaming instruction.

## Further clarifications in the same session

- **Numarius's group** is the power responsible for Leonin dispossession. The Leonin initially welcomed his people after their own homeland was destroyed. Whether this welcome happened to the present Numarius or an earlier generation remains a chronology question. Internal Leonin factions include peace advocates, resistance committed to fighting on, camp institutions, and diaspora networks. Their four working faction names are draft elaborations. Every Leonin carries the lost homeland's flag or emblem; the people are dispersed across the world.
- The author wants to explore **Julius Caesar and triumvirate politics** through the Three Seats. The existing fields/harbor/garrison arrangement remains; no automatic Roman plot, assassination, succession, or final victor is adopted.
- **Lysandria** is an island with two enormous mountains and Amara's home. This corrects the immediately preceding spoken “Jira,” which is not a separate location. Its relationship to Amara's earlier Bakhtari ancestry remains unresolved. Do not invent a family connection between Amara and Alethea.
- **Hanno is a decent fighter and primarily a schemer.** The author identifies Walter White from Breaking Bad as a useful character reference: technical ability, pride, calculation, and capacity for violence. Remove the exceptional fighter/mastery premise from current narrative and profiles.
- **Zaharaz is an ordinary staff**, not a double-bladed spear or a magical source of combat power. Keep its established name and old scene ID so links continue to work.
- Substance complexity is under discussion. The current editorial recommendation retains Ravash and Elysian Dust as the two names a reader tracks, refers to the cure as the treatment, and gives murder preparations no additional branded lore. No merger of the two substances has been adopted.
