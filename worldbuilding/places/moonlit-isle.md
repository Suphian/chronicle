# The Moonlit Isle

ID: `moonlit-isle` · Existing draft location

## Established in current draft

A secluded island off Lysandria. Hanno and Alethea marry here at night. He places a red-and-yellow Elysian flower behind her ear and recognizes its capacity to heal and harm. That flower later becomes the source of Elysian Dust.

Sources: Chapter [Four](../../src/content/chapters/04-gift-of-lysandria.ts), `the-elysian-flower`; Chapter [Eight](../../src/content/chapters/08-a-dark-turn.ts), `elysian-dust`; [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts).

## Interpretation

The island is both an intimate memory and a material source. A later scene set here should not confuse a remembered wedding with a new journey unless the narrative makes that distinction explicit.

## Proposed development

Establish who tends or protects the flower and how it reaches Carthara. A small lived-in community, seasonal cultivation, or harvesting rights could complicate the apparent solitude without changing the wedding's privacy. Hanno's scale of extraction should create a trace or cost.

## Open questions

Is the plant rare everywhere or plentiful locally? Can it grow in Carthara, and in what quantities? The old questionnaire described regional abundance; the current chapter calls it rare. Who witnesses the wedding? What does the island mean to Alethea beyond Hanno's gift?
