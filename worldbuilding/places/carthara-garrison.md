# Carthara Garrison

ID: `carthara-garrison` · Existing draft location within [Carthara](carthara.md)

## Established in current draft

The garrison is the sphere held by [General Tarquin Vael](../characters/vael.md), the Third of the [Three Seats](../factions/the-three-seats.md). Vael commands the city guard, including [Dyia](../characters/dyia.md).

The draft shows guard barracks with bunks, a sentry, and clerical records. At the garrison, Dyia receives a woman seeking the belongings of her dead brother. The institution holds detainees: a chair carrier remains there while his partner waits at Alethea's clinic, until Dyia orders his release. Vael's demand for grain at a council supper makes feeding the garrison an explicit concern alongside the harbor's need for repairs.

## Interpretation

The garrison connects routine employment and administration to coercive power. Pay, lodging, duty records, detention, and a family's demand for a missing key belong to the same institution; its consequences exceed any one officer's intentions.

## Proposed development

Follow a duty clerk, soldier, or relative through a concrete conflict over records, rations, wages, or property. Establish a procedure that can restrain an officer and the circumstances under which it fails.

## Open geography and authority

The garrison's precise position, size, walls, gates, and internal layout are unwritten. The relationship between the barracks shown in earlier chapters and the later garrison offices needs definition before drawing a detailed compound. A map marker denotes the existing institution, not proof of a particular street plan. Its status as a civic force or part of a larger army, and Vael's knowledge of Dyia's unlawful killings, remain open.
