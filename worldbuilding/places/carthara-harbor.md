# Carthara Harbor

ID: `carthara-harbor` · Existing draft location within [Carthara](carthara.md)

## Established in current draft

The harbor is the maritime and customs sphere held by [Magistra Ilvane Corvo](../characters/corvo.md), the Second of the [Three Seats](../factions/the-three-seats.md). Supporting canon gives her control of the docks and customs house. The harbor is a part of Carthara, not a separate country or a fourth Seat.

Hanno leaves through its shipping traffic on Sinna's commission and returns with Alethea. At the quay, a clerk charges him according to a schedule of goods. Later, a boat's missed berth and a disputed parcel in bonded storage complicate his plot against Sinna. Harbor repairs depend on a levy whose payment is tied to duties on Numarius's grain. Corvo also warns that indiscriminate cargo delays will spoil ordinary goods while unusual goods find another departure.

## Interpretation

The harbor makes control over time, access, and records materially valuable. Corvo needs working trade as well as revenue; delaying a cargo can injure people who have no part in her political bargain.

## Proposed development

Give a dock worker, carrier, or customs clerk an independent stake in one disputed shipment. Trace the work and payment needed to repair a berth before using a port closure as an effortless political weapon.

## Open geography and authority

The number of quays, location of the customs house, harbor defenses, shoreline shape, and walking distances to other city places are not established. A map pin should identify the harbor within Carthara without presenting a schematic position as surveyed geography. Corvo's control does not establish knowledge of every shipment or personal responsibility for every inspection.
