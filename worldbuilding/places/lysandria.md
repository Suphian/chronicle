# Lysandria

ID: `lysandria` · Existing draft location

## Established in current draft

An island with **two enormous mountains**, ruled by General Phylios, with rare metals, spear fighters, and a philosophy of harmony. The island and its two mountains are the author's latest geographical decision; older shorthand describing a mountainous kingdom does not make it a mainland state. [Amara](../characters/amara.md) comes from Lysandria. [Alethea](../characters/alethea.md) is also Lysandrian; no family connection between them is established.

Hanno spends five years here in medical and alchemical study and paid work, with occasional basic self-defense practice. He helps save the general's son alongside local practitioners, receives Zaharaz—an ordinary staff given in gratitude—and meets Alethea. His stay does not make him an exceptional warrior. Its coast lies near the separate Moonlit Isle, where they marry. The optional highland legend says a Lysandrian phalanx once failed to hold Bakhtar; that is a claim within the legend, not a surveyed historical border.

Sources: the author's island/origin correction; Chapters [Zero](../../src/content/chapters/00-the-conqueror.ts), [One](../../src/content/chapters/01-market-awnings.ts), [Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), and [Four](../../src/content/chapters/04-gift-of-lysandria.ts); [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts).

Chapter One gives Amara a remembered pottery bench with both mountains visible through its window. This is one local view, not a claim that every settlement sees both peaks or that only two elevations exist on the island.

## Interpretation

Its reputation for balance creates a useful contrast between an admired public ideal and the work required to maintain a military society. Phylios's fairness does not settle whether every Lysandrian institution is fair.

## Proposed development

Give miners, healers, smiths, farmers, potters, and soldiers different stakes in the metals trade. Establish a school or professional community that helped form Alethea, with disagreement rather than unanimous reverence. Amara's former pottery community can have its own customers and disputes. Keep these circles distinct unless a consequential connection earns a place in the story.

An island economy offers useful questions about harbor labor, imported food, ore shipments, and the cost of moving goods up to the mountain settlements. A pass closure or shipping restriction could make Hanno's apparently private purchases politically consequential. These mechanisms are proposals; the author has not fixed the island's size, climate, or degree of self-sufficiency.

## Open questions

Who governs beside the general? How are soldiers supplied and recruited? Where are the harbors, settlements, and training courts relative to the two mountains? What are the mountains called? Their height, geology, and exact shape are not fixed. Neither are the island's world coordinates, distance from Carthara, or sailing time to the wedding island; atlas placement remains schematic.

Chapter Three now shows Hanno arriving by sea, remembering Amara's description of the mountains, and taking the ledge road inland after landing. The crossing's duration and departure port are not fixed. Amara's earlier Bakhtari lineage claim requires reconciliation separately from her adopted Lysandrian origin. Is harmony a shared philosophy, a state ideal, a religion, or several competing traditions? Do not automatically equate it with Hanno's Aetherium Harmonia.

## Adopted Mizan anchor

The November 2023 source map now places Lysandria on its island east of the mainland. Its mapped extent and source elevation are preserved. The two enormous mountains and Amara’s home remain current direction; individual summit heights and harbor sites remain open.
