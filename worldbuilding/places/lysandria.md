# Lysandria

ID: `lysandria` · Existing draft location

## Established in current draft

A mountainous kingdom ruled by General Phylios, with rare metals, spear fighters, and a philosophy of harmony. Hanno trains here for five years, saves the general's son, receives Zaharaz, and meets Alethea. Its coast lies near the Moonlit Isle. The prologue says its phalanx once failed to hold Bakhtar.

Sources: Chapters [Zero](../../src/content/chapters/00-the-conqueror.ts), [Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), and [Four](../../src/content/chapters/04-gift-of-lysandria.ts); [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts).

## Interpretation

Its reputation for balance creates a useful contrast between an admired public ideal and the work required to maintain a military society. Phylios's fairness does not settle whether every Lysandrian institution is fair.

## Proposed development

Give miners, healers, smiths, farmers, and soldiers different stakes in the metals trade. Establish a school or professional community that helped form Alethea, with disagreement rather than unanimous reverence. A pass closure or trade restriction could make Hanno's apparently private purchases politically consequential.

## Open questions

Who governs beside the general? How are soldiers supplied and recruited? Which coast does the kingdom control, and how far is its capital from the wedding island? Is harmony a shared philosophy, a state ideal, a religion, or several competing traditions? Do not automatically equate it with Hanno's Aetherium Harmonia.
