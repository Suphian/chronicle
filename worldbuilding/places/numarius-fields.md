# The Fields of Numarius

ID: `numarius-fields` · Existing draft location

## Established in current draft

Plantations east of Carthara, worked by people compelled through debt. Idris is taken here, dies, and is buried without a name. Numarius controls the fields and orders other indebted families into labor.

Sources: [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts), `numarius-fields`; Chapters [One](../../src/content/chapters/01-market-awnings.ts), `the-fields`, and [Eight](../../src/content/chapters/08-a-dark-turn.ts), `fertilizer`.

## Interpretation

The fields turn an abstract debt into control over bodies and food. They are a working institution in the draft, shown as a landmark on the atlas rather than an abandoned ruin.

## Proposed development

Specify one crop, a water source, the seasonal work cycle, and the accounting rule that prevents release. Develop workers' practical acts of mutual aid, a foreman's divided loyalties, and the route by which food reaches the city. A witness who knew Idris can have a goal that conflicts with Hanno's need for testimony.

## Open questions

How did drought affect the plantations differently from the orchards? Who keeps debtor records, and can accounts be challenged? Are families separated by policy or individual orders? Distinguish narrator fact about Idris's burial from Numarius's degrading account of it. The farm's location on the map does not yet establish travel time.
