# The Tengeri Wastes

ID: `tengeri-wastes` · Existing draft location

## Established in current draft

Wind-carved canyons and jagged cliffs far east of Carthara. Leonin clans inhabit the region. Hanno learns survival, tracking, and archery and trades for herbs, crafted artifacts, and minerals. Chuluun teaches the relation between strength and compassion.

Sources: Chapter [Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), `tengeri-wastes`; [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts); [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts).

## Interpretation

Calling it a waste reflects the existing narration; it need not mean the land lacks a complex economy or history. Skills require teachers and time. Hospitality and trade imply reciprocal relationships that the current travel montage has not yet described.

## Proposed development

Map seasonal routes, water access, and contested collection rights for valuable plants. Different clans may disagree over trade and conservation. Include settlement life and craft work alongside travel. Decide how outsiders compensate hosts and what obligations Hanno may have failed to meet.

## Open questions

Who uses the name “Wastes,” and what names do residents use? How does anyone cross it safely? The older questionnaire's Mongolian horse archery is a historical inspiration, not proof that every clan is nomadic or that the current canyon setting works exactly like a steppe.

The recovered [geography source](../sources/early-geography.md) supplies Erdene Zuu, the Altan and Tsetseg tribes, a lost fifteenth tribe, an empty Golden Yurt, and a dormant Khanate. Preserve these as existing author concepts awaiting integration. Its claim that sparse plants supply all food cannot yet support a large population and should be reconciled with trade, water, cultivation, or a specifically defined fantasy ecology. Its prince Chuluun needs reconciliation with the current elder.
