# Sidrat Al Muntaha

ID: `sidrat-al-muntaha` · Existing draft location

## Established in current draft

A luminous land with sacred lakes, Lake Siraj, moss-covered bridges, and roads edged with bioluminescent plants. Hanno studies the sword alongside philosophy and buys glowing plants and minerals for Sinna.

Sources: Chapter [Three](../../src/content/chapters/03-journeys.ts), `lake-siraj`; [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts); [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts).

## Interpretation

Its light, learning, and sacred landscape can make it more than another stop on a training itinerary. “Sacred” implies custodianship and obligations, but the current text does not say who practices what religion or who owns the resources.

## Proposed development

Connect luminous infrastructure to actual cultivation, maintenance, and limits. A dispute over exporting a medicinal plant could set scholarly exchange against guardianship of the lake. Give a teacher a research question or civic role beyond educating Hanno.

## Open questions

The [recovered geography](../sources/early-geography.md) calls it a kingdom and names the Sidrat Web, Veins of Sidrat, seven sacred lakes, and three major mountains. It specifies bioluminescent algae in Lake Siraj. Those are useful author concepts for integration; the chapter has not yet established their full political and religious system. The source also describes guaranteed safe travel alongside a treacherous mountain pass and a defended hostile frontier: distinguish a civic ideal, an actual magical protection, and geographical danger before expanding this promise.

Does training “beneath” the lake's light mean beside water or physically below it? Review the name's religious provenance in the historical research before elaborating its fictional theology. Do not treat a name with Islamic associations as permission to invent a claim about real Muslim belief.
