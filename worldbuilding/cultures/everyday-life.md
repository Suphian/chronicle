# Everyday life and the work behind the city

Status: working dossier separating actual draft evidence from proposals. Compare particular households and institutions, not a uniform temperament assigned to a species. The [visual direction](../visual-direction.md) belongs to people who build, maintain, inhabit, and sometimes lose its spaces.

## Water and food

**Established in current draft:** Drought destroys Idris's fruit income; Numarius controls plantations with an unspecified staple crop. In the [Tengeri refugee camps](../places/tengeri-wastes.md), bombardment interrupts cooking and care; scarce medicinal plants cannot meet an outsider's requested purchase without depriving residents. Water routes and supplies require labor. See [Carthara](../places/carthara.md) and [the fields](../places/numarius-fields.md).

**Proposed development:** Give one neighborhood a shared cistern and a paid maintenance rota. Choose a staple crop for the plantations and show its route through a weighing yard to bakers or household buyers. During a shortage, a potter needing water to work and a household needing it to drink have competing claims without either becoming a villain.

**Decision and consequence:** Who may restrict supply, and who pays for repairs? If Numarius's fields remain productive during the drought, establish stored water, privileged allocation, or a different growing season. That choice explains both his wealth and the political cost of interrupting food deliveries.

## Work, debt, and ownership

**Established in current draft:** Amara works clay; Sinna appropriates Hanno's labor; the guard enforces debt assignments. Many displaced Leonin are enslaved. [Chuluun](../characters/chuluun.md) has a compulsory labor account himself. Families cannot freely refuse transfers or recover withheld pay; euphemistic labels do not make the work voluntary. Numarius's broader system and Cartharan offices need further administrative detail.

**Proposed development:** Use an ordinary disputed account: a payment has reached a collector but has not reached the ledger. Let a clerk require a witness whose own livelihood depends on the creditor. For Amara, establish access to a shared kiln and what a firing costs; wheel ownership alone cannot guarantee an income.

**Decision and consequence:** Does labor reduce a debt, or do food and lodging charges replenish it? Decide this before writing a release bargain. A rule that benefits landowners still needs workers, records, and enforcement, each a possible source of resistance or evidence.

## Family, care, and education

**Established in current draft:** Amara arranges apprenticeship; Dyia joins the guard. Leonin families teach children, trace transferred relatives, and dispute whether schooling should emphasize a lost home or work available now. Hanno helps his hosts without replacing their expertise. Neither refugee status nor martial tradition makes every resident a fighter. See [the Leonin](../factions/the-leonin.md).

**Proposed development:** Give a neighbor a reciprocal care arrangement with Amara: supervising a child in return for repaired vessels or help at market. Let an apprentice learn accounts before a master allows access to recipes. Literacy may vary by work and language without dividing the population into educated nobles and ignorant laborers.

**Decision and consequence:** What can a family demand from an apprentice's pay, and who can end the contract? Establish Dyia's age and recruitment terms. Education then gives each brother opportunities while drawing labor and attention away from home.

## Medicine and access

**Established in current draft:** Sinna supplies remedies; Hanno and Alethea develop Ravash treatment with consent and follow-up; Alethea treats Dust patients in a clinic with its own account and admission authority. Paying patients and workshop support help fund access. Exact capacity, prices, and treatment limits remain open. See [Alethea](../characters/alethea.md) and the [continuity audit](../continuity.md).

**Proposed development:** Extend the clinic's daily problems through beds, clean linens, follow-up visits, and late shipments. Alethea's existing disagreement with Hanno over authority can grow into a dispute over allocation. Develop the steward's own expertise and relationships, giving care a life beyond the marriage and its founder.

**Decision and consequence:** In this fictional medicine, what does “cured” mean, and what care remains afterward? Distinguish immediate symptom relief from sustained recovery. A citywide improvement must depend on access and ongoing work, not imply that one remedy removes poverty, coercion, or grief.

## Faith, calendars, and remembrance

**Established evidence:** Every Leonin carries the lost homeland's flag or emblem; its meaning varies without erasing that shared connection. Amara comes from Lysandria, the island with two massive mountains; older Bakhtari ancestry remains unresolved. Hanno's Aetherium Harmonia, recovered Solunari worship, and Sidrat traditions are distinct sources, not one religion established by shared language of balance.

**Proposed development:** Before inventing a universal festival, choose how one household marks a death, a working week, and a debt deadline. Let neighbors disagree over the obligation to attend a ceremony while still helping prepare a meal. Institutions need custodians, funding, and arrangements for travelers.

**Decision and consequence:** Whose calendar schedules courts and market closures? Can a worker obtain time for observance? Research particular Islamic historical practices when adapting them; Solunari pilgrimage is not automatically Hajj. Keep [historical research](../ideas/the-pilgrim-ship.md) distinct from fictional theology.

## Infrastructure, craft, and public space

**Established evidence:** Sidrat's bridges and luminous roads require maintenance. Carthara has paid masonry and elite water channels. In Tengeri, repeated bombardment destroys shelter, classrooms, treatment spaces, and records; residents organize rescue and rebuilding while compulsory shifts remove workers. Sophisticated surroundings elsewhere do not establish universal access to protection. See [Sidrat](../places/sidrat-al-muntaha.md).

**Proposed development:** Give cultivated light a maintenance cycle and a trade in replacement growth. Show a rooftop garden's drainage, a cracked public stair, or a shutter-maker's seasonal work. Elaborate brass and stone can express accumulated craft rather than limitless wealth.

**Decision and consequence:** Who repairs a failed bridge, and can residents cross while repairs proceed? If the richest district receives continuous water or lighting, establish its funding and supply. Unequal access then appears in an ordinary journey rather than explanatory speeches.

## News, travel, and belonging

**Established evidence:** Leonin diaspora communities live throughout the world. The [Scattered Hearths](../factions/the-scattered-hearths.md) send aid, testimony, and reunion offers. Hanno's travel paper permits exit while residents' permits are refused. He witnesses an inhabited camp attacked, then reads official lies denying civilians and calling coerced labor voluntary. Other powers repeat those claims; distrust of propaganda does not settle every allegation against fighters.

**Proposed development:** Track one letter with a named carrier, a fare, and a reason for delay. Distinguish a merchant's report from a guard notice and a patient's firsthand account. In a multilingual port, an interpreter or scribe can possess useful authority without secretly controlling every exchange.

**Decision and consequence:** Which crossings close by season, and who can afford passage? Set these limits before making Hanno continuously aware of Dyia's movements. Delayed information should change decisions for ordinary families as well as investigators.
