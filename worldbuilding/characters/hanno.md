# Hanno Averroes

ID: `hanno` · Draft endpoint: Chapter Ten · Alive · Full-story spoilers

## Established in current draft

Hanno is the eldest son of [Idris](adil.md) and [Amara](amara.md), a half-elf raised in Carthara. Amara comes from [Lysandria](../places/lysandria.md), the island with two enormous mountains; the earlier Bakhtari ancestry claim remains to be reconciled with that origin. He is 115 in the character file, with the full chronology still unsettled. [Sinna](virello.md) exploits his alchemical work. Hanno's travels bring practical knowledge, relationships, and competent self-defense, not extraordinary fighting ability. Five years of study and work on Lysandria lead to helping save Phylios's son, the gift of **Zaharaz, an ordinary wooden traveling staff**, and marriage to [Alethea](alethea.md).

His treatment helps people recover from Ravash. He later engineers Sinna's death after Sinna murders Amara. After Numarius's banquet he develops Elysian Dust, conceals it from Alethea, and obstructs [Dyia's](dyia.md) investigation. He finds Alethea dead, looks in a mirror, and opens the door to Dyia.

His stated faith is Aetherium Harmonia. He gardens, avoids mirrors, and needs public gratitude even while passing judgment on the city. These are specific contradictions in his behavior, not evidence that every act is deceitful.

## Adopted trade premise — 2026-09-07

Hanno's discoveries and innovations enrich Sinna. His travels also establish trade routes and working relationships from which Sinna's business benefits. Contacts know Hanno as the person who did the work, rather than knowing only his master's name. Chapter Two now links repeat orders for his improved remedy to Sinna's purchase of more space. Chapter Three shows Hanno negotiating recurring supply, packing, and carriage terms. Chapter Five shows the resulting stock and property, then a dispute over a supplier letter addressed to Hanno. These are initial scenes for a relationship that can expand with the travel arc.

That network gives Hanno a credible source of relationships beyond Carthara, not automatic loyalty or limitless reach. A later contact helping him independently of Sinna remains **proposed development**: no particular favor, willing accomplice, or role in the Dust trade has been adopted. Each person's livelihood, obligations, and knowledge should determine whether they help, refuse, or demand terms.

## Interpretation

**Author-selected character reference:** Walter White in *Breaking Bad*. Use the combination of technical ability, calculation, injured pride, self-justification, and capacity for killing. This is a guide to how Hanno exerts power, not a request to copy a plot, diagnosis, family, or chemistry. His decisions should depend on materials, information, money, relationships, and other people's choices. He cannot solve the consequences through sudden martial supremacy.

He wants control over the conditions that made his family disposable. Recognition becomes his proof that he is no longer powerless. His strongest skill is turning knowledge into exchange; his weakness is treating affection as another transaction he can secure. His moral vocabulary lets him describe punishment as restoration. The market is not collectively guilty merely because he believes it is.

He loves Dyia and Alethea, yet confuses keeping them uninformed with keeping them safe. His need is to acknowledge other people's agency and the limits of his authority, not simply to forgive Numarius. Grief at the garden does not by itself constitute restitution or redemption. His ability to plan is not omniscience: people can refuse a bargain, misunderstand an instruction, retain a receipt, or choose something he failed to consider.

## Proposed development

Use the early questionnaire's dream of a fruit cart as a modest private ambition he repeatedly postpones. Give him a chance to fund independent treatment without controlling it; his reaction would reveal whether healing or ownership matters more. Let a trusted merchant refuse him for a reason he understands. Define limits to his money, surveillance, and skill so success requires choices rather than effortless superiority.

## Open questions and continuity

Could his evidence against Sinna survive a hostile hearing? What does the broker suspect beyond Hanno's claim to finance supply? Additional cultivation and partial knowledge channels now exist in the prose, but their quantities, locations, capacity, and specific vulnerabilities remain open. Resolve the 115-year age against living childhood witnesses. He has never visited Bakhtar; the ancestral parallel is an earlier draft claim, not an established revelation he receives. Amara and Alethea sharing an island of origin establishes no kinship. His response to Dyia remains unwritten.
