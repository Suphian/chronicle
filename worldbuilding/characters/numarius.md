# Lord Numarius

ID: `numarius` · First Seat, holder of the fields · Alive at the draft endpoint

## Established in current draft

Numarius owns plantations east of Carthara and the debtors compelled to work them. His agents take [Adil](adil.md) and later the Averroes household. He profits from Ravash with the other Seats, celebrates Hanno publicly, tolerates Virello's accusations, and recognizes danger after Virello's death. At the banquet he deliberately humiliates Hanno with Adil's fate. Later the Dust threatens elite stability and productivity; he presses Dyia to stop it while [Corvo](corvo.md) and [Vael](vael.md) consider his weakness.

Sources: Chapters [One](../../src/content/chapters/01-market-awnings.ts), [Five](../../src/content/chapters/05-the-cure.ts), [Six](../../src/content/chapters/06-shadows-and-accusations.ts), [Eight](../../src/content/chapters/08-a-dark-turn.ts), [Nine](../../src/content/chapters/09-shadows-closing-in.ts).

## Interpretation

He wants prosperity to appear dependent on his patronage. Public humiliation restores hierarchy when Hanno's usefulness starts looking like independence. His constraint is that land cannot feed his power without transport, labor, and armed enforcement. He can fear disorder sincerely while treating other people's suffering as an acceptable price.

## Proposed development

Specify a material responsibility he can point to—grain reserves, irrigation, or seasonal credit—and show the coercive terms attached. Give him an adviser who can accurately warn him, so his failures arise from priorities and choices. A dependency on Corvo's imports would explain both their cooperation and a concrete reason she could move against him.

## Open questions and continuity

What grants the First Seat primacy? Are debtor laborers legally property, indentured, or held through fraudulent accounts? Who succeeds him? Why does a man wary of Hanno risk the banquet insult? Supply the political calculation rather than treating cruelty as his only trait. The early source's king and empire remain unresolved in the current setting.
