# Magistra Ilvane Corvo

ID: `corvo` · Second Seat, holder of the harbor · Alive at the draft endpoint

## Established in current draft

Corvo controls Carthara's docks and customs house. She profits from Ravash; `CLAUDE.md` identifies her as the Silken Chain's secret head. That is author knowledge, not something Dyia has discovered. The codex notes that the Chain is never caught moving Ravash by sea. By Chapter Nine she and [Vael](vael.md) are treating [Numarius](numarius.md) as a liability.

## Current visual portrayal — 2026-09-07

Corvo is a Black woman with deep brown skin. Her portrait should convey an experienced magistrate through composed bearing, refined civic dress, and the working surroundings of the harbor's customs authority. She belongs among the city's powerful public figures as fully as Black residents belong among its families, traders, and learned professionals.

## Interpretation

Her power is control over circulation and records rather than ownership of every object. She can benefit from a predictable city without feeling personal loyalty to either colleague. The cure removes illicit revenue; the Dust undermines confidence in the institutions that make trade possible. Those are different threats and may call for different responses.

## Proposed development

Give her a legitimate constituency among shippers or dock workers and a policy that helps them while tightening dependence. Define which information reaches her and which is lost through delegated authority. A proposed pilgrimage atrocity could confront her with testimony, protection failures, and competing claims over confiscated cargo; it should not automatically make her its perpetrator.

## Open questions and continuity

Why has Dyia not linked the Chain to customs? Does she recognize Hanno's cargo, suspect him, or mistake his network for a rival's? None is established. How can someone move contraband through her harbor without a trace? Decide the practical limit or bargain before using her as an all-knowing puppet master. Her family, origins, age, and route to office are open.
