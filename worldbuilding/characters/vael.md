# General Tarquin Vael

ID: `vael` · Third Seat, holder of the garrison · Alive at the draft endpoint

## Established in current draft

Vael commands Carthara's city guard, including [Dyia](dyia.md). He believes order is worth any price. The garrison shares in Ravash profits alongside fields and harbor. As the Dust destabilizes Carthara, he and [Corvo](corvo.md) begin considering [Numarius](numarius.md) expendable.

Sources: [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts), `vael`; Chapters [Five](../../src/content/chapters/05-the-cure.ts) and [Nine](../../src/content/chapters/09-shadows-closing-in.ts); [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md).

## Interpretation

He can frame corruption as the price of maintaining an institution he considers indispensable. Dyia's competence is valuable, but his investigation could expose the arrangement supporting the guard. Vael therefore has reasons both to encourage the inquiry and to limit it. Those motives do not yet establish specific orders he has given.

## Proposed development

Specify what his soldiers are owed and what happens when wages or grain fail. Give him a decision where protecting public order costs the other Seats money. Show a boundary he actually respects and a later pressure that threatens it. A commanding officer who sometimes constrains violence will make Dyia's violations a more meaningful choice.

## Open questions and continuity

Does he know about Dyia's unlawful killings, and would he protect, punish, or exploit him? Who commissions or removes a guard captain? Is the garrison a civic force or part of a larger army? He has no established personal military history yet. Do not fabricate a past war as the explanation for every belief without marking it proposed.
