# Master Virello

ID: `virello` · Draft endpoint: Chapter Ten · Dead

## Established in current draft

A Cartharan apothecary who trains [Hanno](hanno.md), appropriates his discoveries, and dispatches him for ingredients. He understands alchemical precision well enough to teach it. When Hanno's independent cure eclipses him, Virello murders [Amara](amara.md) with disguised Ravash and spreads accusations against the cure and its maker. Hanno baits him with a supposedly valuable shipment; Virello steals it and dies demonstrating it publicly.

Sources: Chapters [One](../../src/content/chapters/01-market-awnings.ts), [Two](../../src/content/chapters/02-bitter-apprenticeship.ts), [Five](../../src/content/chapters/05-the-cure.ts), and [Six](../../src/content/chapters/06-shadows-and-accusations.ts).

## Interpretation

His professional identity rests on being the indispensable master. Hanno's success threatens both income and the story Virello tells about his own merit. He treats apprenticeship as ownership. Genuine knowledge makes the exploitation more credible; he need not be secretly incompetent to have stolen work.

## Proposed development

Give him an actual contribution Hanno still uses and a defensible teaching practice he later abuses. Establish the economic arrangement that lets him own the apprentice's output. A former apprentice could remember both effective care and theft. Make his public demonstration an understandable professional gamble under pressure, not sudden stupidity inserted to solve the plot.

## Open questions and continuity

How does Hanno prove the murder? Why does Amara trust Virello after Hanno's independence? Which authority certifies remedies or investigates deaths? The current draft does not establish Virello as a Silken Chain officer, nor confirm the questionnaire's claim he originally introduced Amara to Ravash. His death by poisoned shipment is a deliberate revision; do not restore an earlier direct killing by accident.
