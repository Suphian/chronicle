# Rukhsana

ID: `rukhsana` · Historical figure within the fictional world · Dead by the present era

## Established in current draft

An elven-blooded Bakhtari flute-player. She counts [Iskandar's](iskandar.md) army from a cave mouth and tells him the number. They marry underground in the highland manner. The Averroes family descends from their line through [Amara](amara.md).

Sources: Chapter [Zero](../../src/content/chapters/00-the-conqueror.ts), `rukhsana` and `the-line`; [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts).

## Interpretation

Her first recorded act is observation stated without deference. That suggests confidence and local knowledge; it does not prove she is a spy, princess, magical oracle, or representative of all Bakhtari. The current legend privileges what she means to the conqueror and his descendants, leaving her own purposes largely unwritten.

## Proposed development

Give her a reason to approach or confront him that predates romance: a named community's demand or a contested decision about remaining in a threatened valley. Give her a relationship with someone who opposes the marriage for a specific reason. Let her continue acting after Iskandar's death; what she preserves or refuses may shape the song the descendants inherit.

## Open questions and continuity

What did she want from marriage, and what alternatives were available under conquest? What power, if any, did she hold independently? Her lifespan, death, children, and role in succession are unknown. Elven ancestry is not shorthand for a culture's spirituality or timelessness. Do not make her merely the reward for Iskandar's cultural transformation or a bloodline delivery mechanism.
