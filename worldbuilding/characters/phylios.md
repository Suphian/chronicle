# General Phylios

ID: `phylios` · Leader of Lysandria · Alive in his last narrated appearance

## Established in current draft

Phylios is grey-eyed, stern, and fair. He rules Lysandria and values self-mastery. [Hanno](hanno.md) trains among its warriors for five years. When Phylios's young son falls ill and other healers cannot cure him, Hanno works overnight and saves him. Phylios presents Zaharaz publicly in gratitude.

Sources: Chapter [Four](../../src/content/chapters/04-gift-of-lysandria.ts), `ancient-guardians` and `the-spear`; [`world.ts`](../../src/content/world.ts), `lysandria`; [`codex.ts`](../../src/content/codex.ts).

## Interpretation

He is evidence against Hanno's claim that all authority is corrupt. Fairness should not mean that he has no competing loyalties: saving his son creates personal gratitude that may later conflict with his responsibility as ruler. His teachings matter most when they constrain his own actions.

## Proposed development

Place him in a dispute between people who train for war and people who supply them. If he learns of the Dust, a demand for evidence before punishing Hanno could be both principled and painfully self-interested. Develop the son as a person with an age and wishes before using him as a diplomatic link or hostage.

## Open questions and continuity

What makes a general the ruler, and who can oppose him lawfully? What does he know about Hanno after departure? The son's name, illness, and present age are unspecified. The spear's name is established; the claim that “Zaharaz” reads identically backward is a spelling contradiction, not a reason to quietly rename the artifact.
