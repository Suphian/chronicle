# Video

Drop generated clips here (Runway, Veo, Sora, Kling, Pika — anything that exports MP4/WebM).
Reference from a scene as `video: "/video/prologue/intro.mp4"`.

Tips: export 1920x1080 or 1080x1920, H.264 MP4, under ~15 MB per clip, and keep clips
short (5–12 s). They loop silently behind the text; sound comes from the audio layer.
